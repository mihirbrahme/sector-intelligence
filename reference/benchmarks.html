Asset Carriage / Truckload Fleet

Operating Model Definition: Model 4. Asset-heavy motor carriers running company tractors, trailers, and driver pools. Primary operational measure is Operating Ratio (OR) (Operating Expenses ÷ Operating Revenue).

Critical Segmentation Rule: Irregular-Route Over-the-Road (One-Way OTR) and Dedicated Contract Carriage operate on fundamentally divergent commercial models. Blending them into a single median produces an unusable metric. They are benchmarked separately below.

Sub-sector 4A: Irregular-Route Over-the-Road (One-Way OTR)

Operates on spot and annual routing guide contracts across an open network. Network density, empty miles %, and spot market rates dictate margins. - Unit of work: the loaded mile / linehaul trip - Primary metric: Operating Ratio (OR), revenue per loaded mile, empty miles % - Peer set: Heartland Express (pure-play OTR), Marten Transport (Truckload excl. Dedicated), Werner Enterprises (One-Way Truckload), Schneider National (Network Truckload), Knight-Swift (One-Way OTR), PAM Transportation.
