How it is built

  • Air Freight Services: Consolidation and procurement of wholesale airline cargo capacity; export volume heavily driven by Asian manufacturing and hyperscaler AI server shipments.
  • Ocean Freight and Ocean Services: Ocean consolidation, container forwarding, and order management; unit volume measured in 40ft/20ft container loads.
  • Customs Brokerage & Other Services: Fee-for-service customs clearance, duty drawback, tariff classification, and trade compliance advisory. Provides stable gross profit margins uncoupled from underlying freight rate volatility.
  • Branch-level compensation structure: Field personnel and executive management receive modest base salaries coupled with an invariant share of branch-level operating profit, mechanically flexing personnel expense with operating performance.

The shape of the numbers

Per, freight forwarders are evaluated on net revenue (gross revenues less carrier transportation costs), not gross revenues. The core efficiency metric is the conversion margin (operating income / net revenue).

What to look for

Failures that recur in this model and would show in this operator's numbers before they show anywhere else.

Billing exception leakage. DSO widens without a change in customer mix or payment terms.

Detention and demurrage under-recovery. Charges absorbed rather than contested or passed through, in periods when port and terminal dwell is rising.

Purchased-transportation accrual failure. Purchased transportation expense and accounts payable are both understated, discovered at a year-end close rather than at any period close during the year, and the restatement then widens backwards into prior years rather than staying confined to the year in which it was found.

Fragmented carrier master after acquisition. Onboarding cycle time grows after an acquisition closes; duplicate carrier records; compliance exceptions rise.

Quote-to-book manual handoff. Loads per rep per day flat or falling while volume grows.

Post-restructuring capability gap. Layoffs announced, then volume recovers, then service metrics deteriorate. All three, in that order. The third is the one that makes it this mechanism, and it is the one most often missing from disclosure.
