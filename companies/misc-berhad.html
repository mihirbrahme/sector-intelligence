How it is built

  • Gas Assets & Solutions: Operates a large fleet of LNG carriers and very large ethane carriers (VLECs) predominantly under long-term time charters to PETRONAS and international LNG portfolio players.
  • Petroleum & Product Shipping (AET): Operates crude oil tankers (VLCC, Suezmax, Aframax), clean product tankers, and dynamic-positioning shuttle tankers serving Atlantic Basin and Brazilian offshore fields.
  • Offshore Business: Owns and operates floating production, storage, and offloading (FPSO) and floating storage and offloading (FSO) units under multi-year field availability contracts.
  • Marine & Heavy Engineering (MHB): Operates the Pasir Gudang shipyard in Johor, Malaysia, performing offshore platform fabrication and vessel dry-docking/repair.

What to look for

Failures that recur in this model and would show in this operator's numbers before they show anywhere else.

Dry-docking bunching removes revenue days. Several scheduled dockings fall into the same period, reducing revenue days and utilisation before visible repair cost rises.

Off-hire and technical downtime convert owned fleet into idle capital. Vessel count is stable, but available days, contracted days or revenue days fall because ships are technically unavailable.

Environmental compliance capex forces retrofit, speed or scrap decision. Environmental rules create capex or operating constraints that are not matched by freight demand or customer rate increases.

Billing exception leakage. DSO widens without a change in customer mix or payment terms.

Post-restructuring capability gap. Layoffs announced, then volume recovers, then service metrics deteriorate. All three, in that order. The third is the one that makes it this mechanism, and it is the one most often missing from disclosure.

Purchased-transportation accrual failure. Purchased transportation expense and accounts payable are both understated, discovered at a year-end close rather than at any period close during the year, and the restatement then widens backwards into prior years rather than staying confined to the year in which it was found.
