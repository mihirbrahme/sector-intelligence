Samudera Shipping Line

Samudera Shipping Line is a Singapore-listed regional shipping operator. Its dominant segment is container shipping, with smaller bulk, tanker and logistics segments.

Operating model
International container liner and feeder shipping, Dry bulk ocean logistics and chartering
Unit of work
the loaded container carried, normally TEU

What to look for

Failures that recur in this model and would show in this operator's numbers before they show anywhere else.

Charter-in rate exposure locked at the wrong point in the cycle. Charter hire per revenue day, TEU or tonne rises after market freight rates turn down. The operator carries high-cost chartered tonnage into a weaker revenue market.

Slot under-utilisation hidden by aggregate TEU growth. Liftings rise or hold, but revenue per TEU, cost per TEU or schedule contribution deteriorates because added boxes are on weak lanes, backhaul legs or feeder relay cargo.

Port congestion breaks liner rotation integrity. Vessels miss planned port windows, bunch at terminals, skip calls or require extra ships to keep weekly service.

Bunker and carbon pass-through clause mismatch. Fuel, bunker or carbon cost rises faster than realised surcharge revenue, even where contracts say the cost is recoverable.

Billing exception leakage. DSO widens without a change in customer mix or payment terms.

Post-restructuring capability gap. Layoffs announced, then volume recovers, then service metrics deteriorate. All three, in that order. The third is the one that makes it this mechanism, and it is the one most often missing from disclosure.
