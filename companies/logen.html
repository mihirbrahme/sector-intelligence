How it is built

  • Parcel Courier Network (Logen Express): Fourth-largest parcel courier operator in South Korea (~7–8% market share), operating central sorting hubs (including Icheon, 옥천/Okcheon, and newly expanded regional terminals) linked to hundreds of franchised local sub-terminals and independent owner-operator delivery drivers (지입차주).
  • Customer and Cargo Mix: Specialises in small-to-medium online merchants (SOHOs), C2C returns/shipments, agricultural produce, and Daemyung Chemical Group captive fashion/apparel e-commerce fulfilment.
  • Corporate Merger and Demerger Timeline: 1. 2021: Cowell Fashion (A033290), backed by Daemyung Chemical Group, acquired 100% of Logen Co., Ltd. from Baring Private Equity Asia for ~KRW 340bn. 2. December 2023: Cowell Fashion executed a horizontal spin-off (human division) of its fashion/apparel business into newly listed Pond Group Co., Ltd., leaving A033290 anchored by Logen's parcel operations. 3. December 2025: A033290 completed a statutory absorption merger with wholly owned subsidiary Logen Co., Ltd. and changed its listed company name to LOGEN Co., Ltd.
