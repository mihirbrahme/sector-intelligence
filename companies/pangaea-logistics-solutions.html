Pangaea Logistics Solutions

Pangaea Logistics Solutions is a dry bulk ocean logistics operator. It combines cargo contracts, owned vessels, chartered-in tonnage and port services.

Operating model
Dry bulk ocean logistics and chartering
Unit of work
the loaded voyage

What to look for

Failures that recur in this model and would show in this operator's numbers before they show anywhere else.

Charter-in rate exposure locked at the wrong point in the cycle. Charter hire per revenue day, TEU or tonne rises after market freight rates turn down. The operator carries high-cost chartered tonnage into a weaker revenue market.

Off-hire and technical downtime convert owned fleet into idle capital. Vessel count is stable, but available days, contracted days or revenue days fall because ships are technically unavailable.

Charter counterparty default interrupts cash and voyage control. A charterer, cargo counterparty or sub-charterer fails to pay, disputes hire, or defaults while a vessel is committed.

Billing exception leakage. DSO widens without a change in customer mix or payment terms.

Post-restructuring capability gap. Layoffs announced, then volume recovers, then service metrics deteriorate. All three, in that order. The third is the one that makes it this mechanism, and it is the one most often missing from disclosure.

Purchased-transportation accrual failure. Purchased transportation expense and accounts payable are both understated, discovered at a year-end close rather than at any period close during the year, and the restatement then widens backwards into prior years rather than staying confined to the year in which it was found.
