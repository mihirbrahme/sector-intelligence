In 90 seconds

What it does

Comprehensive maritime enterprise resource planning (ERP) platform dominating technical ship management, planned maintenance, and marine accounting

Where it is used

International container liner and feeder shipping, Dry bulk ocean logistics and chartering, Energy shipping: LNG, crude, product, offshore FPSO/FSO

What it does against the unit of work

Danaos Software executes operations against the shipboard machinery maintenance hour, spare part requisition, dry-docking work item, crew payroll slip, and vessel operating expense (OPEX) voucher.

The platform provides an end-to-end technical, operational, and financial ERP for shipowners and technical ship managers: - Planned Maintenance System (PMS): Schedules and records class-mandated maintenance on main engines, auxiliary generators, pumps, and deck machinery based on running hours or calendar intervals. - Dry-Docking Management: Compiles detailed repair specifications, issues competitive tenders to global shipyards, and tracks job progress and cost variances during dry-dock overhauls. - Spare Parts & Purchasing: Directs the requisitioning of marine spares from global ship chandlers and equipment OEMs, tracking inventory from shore warehouses to vessel holds. - Crew Management & Payroll: Manages seafarer documentation, STCW certification compliance, crew embarkation/repatriation travel, and multi-currency maritime payroll. - Marine Financial Accounting: Governs vessel-level P&Ls, running cost (OPEX) accounting, charter hire reconciliation, and statutory audit compliance.

Models and sub-sectors

  • Operating models: Model 10 (Dry Bulk Shipping), Model 11 (Tanker Shipping), and Model 12 (Container Shipping Line).
  • Sub-sectors: Technical ship management companies, independent commercial shipowners, and containership charter-owners.
  • Geographic stronghold: Dominant in the Greek shipping cluster (Piraeus)—the world's largest commercial shipowning community—alongside Cyprus, Singapore, and Northern Europe.
  • Commercial model: Enterprise licensing per vessel managed, annual software maintenance agreements, and professional implementation fees.

Market position and customer profile

Danaos is one of the world's most established maritime ERP providers, competing with DNV (ShipManager), Kongsberg, and ABS Nautical Systems.

  • Market footprint: Installed across more than 500 shipping companies managing over 5,000 ocean-going vessels globally.
  • Customer profile: Mid-market to mega-shipowners and third-party technical ship managers managing fleets from 10 to over 100 deep-sea vessels.
  • Heritage: Developed in close operational collaboration with Danaos Corporation (NYSE: DAC), one of the world's largest containership charter-owners.

Where it is weak

Danaos ERP implementations involve complex shipboard technical workflows: 1. Ship-to-shore synchronization challenges: Vessel systems run on local shipboard servers and synchronize with shore headquarters via satellite links (VSAT). Bandwidth constraints and latency can cause database replication conflicts. 2. Master data prerequisite load: Setting up a vessel in the PMS requires cataloguing thousands of machinery components, serial numbers, running-hour counters, and spare part cross-references. 3. User interface density: The legacy desktop applications are feature-dense and complex, requiring comprehensive training for shore superintendents and chief engineers. 4. Commercial chartering gap: Danaos is a technical ship management and accounting ERP; it does not replace commercial voyage estimation and chartering platforms like Veson IMOS.

Data model and integration

  • Database: Relational Microsoft SQL Server or Oracle database architecture supporting bi-directional asynchronous replication between shipboard servers and shore offices.
  • Maritime standards: Fully aligned with International Safety Management (ISM) Code, International Ship and Port Facility Security (ISPS) Code, and major classification society survey rules (ABS, DNV, Lloyd's Register).
  • Supply chain protocols: Supports MTML (Marine Trading Markup Language) and IMPA/ISSA standard marine coding for electronic spare parts procurement.
  • Web portal: DanaosONE platform provides cloud-based collaboration connecting shipowners with suppliers, classification societies, and banks.

Ownership and consolidation

  • Founded in 1986 in Piraeus, Greece.
  • Privately held and managed by maritime IT veterans and the Coustas family (founders of Danaos Corporation).
  • Has maintained organic growth and independence within the global maritime software ecosystem.

What tends to go wrong

  • dry-docking bunching removes revenue days: Danaos Dry-Docking software compiles technical specifications and tracks daily shipyard task completions. By managing critical-path shipyard tasks (hull blasting, tailshaft surveys, ballast tank coatings), it helps superintendents prevent dry-dock overruns that remove vessels from commercial service.
  • off-hire and technical downtime convert owned fleet into idle capital: Danaos PMS enforces preventive maintenance on critical machinery. Equipment failures on main engines or diesel generators trigger immediate off-hire clauses; rigorous PMS adherence in Danaos provides the audit trail to maintain insurance coverage and charterparty performance warranties.
  • fleet age capex cliff raises maintenance before replacement is funded (Fleet age capex cliff): Tracks vessel OPEX per day, machinery overhaul costs, and spare parts consumption by vessel age, giving shipowners the empirical data needed to evaluate whether to invest in life-extension overhauls or scrap older tonnage.
  • environmental compliance capex forces retrofit, speed or scrap decision (Environmental compliance capex): Manages the technical implementation and maintenance of carbon-reduction retrofits (propeller boss cap fins, Mewis ducts, silicon hull coatings, scrubber systems).

What is claimed, and what is corroborated

  • Vendor claims: Danaos claims complete technical control, automated classification compliance, and up to 20% reduction in vessel operating expenses.
  • Maritime press and industry recognition: Reporting in Lloyd's List and TradeWinds consistently highlights Danaos as the foundational software backbone of the Greek and international merchant fleet, recognized for robust financial accounting and technical compliance rigor.
