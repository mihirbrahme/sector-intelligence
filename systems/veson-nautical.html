What it does against the unit of work

Veson Nautical executes operations against the vessel fixture, voyage estimate, bunker fuel stem, Statement of Facts (SOF), laytime calculation, and charter hire invoice.

Its flagship platform, the Veson IMOS Platform (VIP), governs the commercial lifecycle of ocean shipping: - Pre-Fixture & Voyage Estimation: Calculates projected Time Charter Equivalent (TCE) rates per day across potential spot voyages, modeling cargo size, canal tolls, sea route distances, and bunker consumption. - Fixture & Charterparty Administration: Records executed fixture terms, charterparty clauses, recap agreements, and vessel warranties. - Voyage Operations: Tracks real-time vessel positions, noon reports, weather routing, and bunker procurement stems. - Financial Settlement & Laytime: Parses Statement of Facts (SOF) port logs, calculates contractual laytime, generates demurrage claims, and issues freight and hire invoices. - Environmental & Carbon Compliance: Models vessel carbon intensity (IMO CII) and calculates European Union Emissions Trading System (EU ETS) maritime allowance obligations per voyage.

Models and sub-sectors

  • Operating models: Model 10 (Dry Bulk Shipping), Model 11 (Tanker Shipping), Model 12 (Container Shipping Line), and Model 13 (Ro-Ro).
  • Sub-sectors: Capesize/Panamax bulk carriers, crude and product tankers, chemical gas carriers, global commodity trading desks (Cargill, Bunge, Trafigura, Glencore), and ocean disponent owners.
  • Commercial model: Enterprise SaaS subscription model based on active vessel fleet count and functional modules, plus professional integration fees.

Market position and customer profile

Veson Nautical is the undisputed global commercial standard in ocean freight chartering and commercial voyage management.

  • Market position: Deployed by over 300 shipowners, operators, and chartering desks, managing tens of thousands of commercial vessels.
  • Customer profile: Major industrial shipping lines (Oldendorff Carriers, Pacific Basin, Scorpio Tankers, Euronav) and multinational commodity charterers.
  • Industry dominance: Equivalent to what Bloomberg is to securities trading: IMOS voyage estimates and contracts are the universal commercial currency across global shipbroking and operations.

Where it is weak

Veson's focus on commercial operations creates specific technical and functional boundaries: 1. Not a technical ship management system: Veson does not manage shipboard machinery planned maintenance (PMS), class surveys, crew payroll, or spare parts procurement. Those functions require specialized technical ERPs (Danaos, ABS Nautical Systems). 2. Noon report data quality dependency: Voyage P&L accuracy depends entirely on daily vessel noon reports transmitted from shipboard engineers. If vessel crews report inaccurate bunker consumption or engine RPMs, voyage margin calculations distort. 3. ERP integration friction: Interfacing VIP financial ledgers with corporate general ledgers (SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics) requires complex custom middleware and multi-currency reconciliation.

Data model and integration

  • Architecture: Cloud-hosted multi-tenant SaaS architecture (VIP) accessible via modern web browsers and mobile apps.
  • Data entities: Vessel Characteristics, Speed/Consumption Curves, Port Tariffs, Distance Tables, Bunker Grades, Charterparty Clauses, and SOF Events.
  • Consolidated data mesh: Incorporates real-time satellite AIS tracking, Oceanbolt bulk commodity flow intelligence, and VesselsValue vessel valuation data.
  • APIs: Provides comprehensive REST APIs for bi-directional data flow with corporate financial systems, bunker trading platforms, and onboard ship communication servers.

What tends to go wrong

  • detention and demurrage under-recovery: Demurrage is the core financial dispute in tramp shipping. Veson's laytime calculation engine parses Statement of Facts timestamps, rain delays, and charterparty exceptions (e.g. NOR validities, strike clauses) to compute legally binding laytime and demurrage claims, preventing revenue leakage.
  • charter-in rate exposure locked at the wrong point in the cycle (Charter-in rate exposure locked at wrong point in cycle): Vessel operators use Veson voyage estimation to evaluate whether taking in third-party tonnage on time-charter yields positive spot spread. Miscalculating voyage duration or bunker burn in Veson locks in negative TCE margins.
  • bunker and carbon pass-through clause mismatch: Under new environmental regulations (IMO CII, EU ETS), shipowners must surrender carbon allowances for voyages touching European ports. Veson models carbon exposure per charterparty clause, ensuring allowances are billed to charterers rather than absorbed by the owner.

What is claimed, and what is corroborated

  • Vendor claims: Veson claims to power 500 million tonnes of annual cargo movement, eliminate chartering errors, and optimize every maritime dollar.
  • Maritime press and industry benchmarks: Reporting in TradeWinds and Lloyd's List confirms Veson IMOS as the universal commercial standard across dry bulk and tanker operators, noting that its recent acquisitions give it an unprecedented monopoly over maritime intelligence and operational data.
