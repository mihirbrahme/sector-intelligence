In 90 seconds

What it does

Information services and data consortia that aggregate freight transaction records and load board activity to publish rate benchmarks, spot indices, and capacity metrics

Where it is used

Truckload Brokerage, Freight Forwarding / NVOCC, Inland liquid-bulk marine / tank barge

What it does against the unit of work

Rate and market data providers process the historical freight transaction, load tender confirmation, container spot booking, and load board posting.

The provider ingests transactional freight invoices, rate confirmations, and electronic load postings from participating shippers, brokers, and carriers.

Statistical engines clean raw transaction feeds, filter statistical outliers, and aggregate freight costs across geographic lanes and equipment types.

The output is a published benchmark. Examples include a median rate per mile, a container spot index, or a load-to-truck ratio.

The market separates into two geographic and modal categories:

  • North American Road Freight: DAT One / DAT iQ (Roper Technologies), Truckstop.com, FreightWaves SONAR. These platforms dominate domestic truckload spot pricing, contract rate analytics, and capacity balances.
  • Global Ocean and Air Freight: Xeneta, Freightos (Baltic Index - FBX), Drewry Shipping Consultants, Transport Intelligence (Ti Insight). Xeneta operates a benchmarking consortium crowdsourcing container rates. Freightos calculates spot indices from marketplace transactions. Drewry and Ti provide market assessments and macroeconomic forecasts.

Work it carries

  • Dynamic pricing and buy rate guidance: Injecting lane rate benchmarks directly into broker quoting screens and shipper procurement workflows.
  • Benchmarking data consortium: Providing anonymized peer rate comparisons in exchange for contributed transactional freight data.

Models and geographies

  • Operating models: Model 1 (Brokerage), Model 2 (Forwarding), shipper / BCO in-house transportation management — a buyer type, not one of the 13 operating models.
  • Geographies:
  • North America: DAT Solutions and Truckstop dominate domestic road freight. DAT is the universal operational currency for US truckload spot trading.
  • Global Ocean & Air: Xeneta and Drewry lead international container shipping benchmarks. Freightos provides public indices for spot container movements.
  • Strategic Macro: Ti Insight and Drewry provide international logistics market sizing and contract logistics benchmarking.
  • Commercial model: Annual subscription SaaS licenses based on user seats, lane lookup volume, or API query calls. Consortia platforms (Xeneta) offer tiered discounts to shippers who contribute continuous freight spend data feeds.

Where it is weak

Market data platforms provide historical averages rather than forward-looking guarantees. In sudden market dislocations (severe weather or port strikes), published indices lag real carrier asking rates by days or weeks.

Aggregated benchmarks obscure micro-level operational variables. A published rate per mile cannot capture excessive facility dwell time, poor shipper dock reputation, or difficult accessorial requirements.

Consortium data exhibits inherent selection bias. Shippers contributing high freight volumes secure lower baseline rates than smaller spot shippers, distorting index medians for mid-market users.

When procurement teams blindly enforce index averages during capacity squeezes, carriers reject load tenders, driving freight into expensive spot auctions.

Who owns the preparation

Rate data providers refuse customer data cleansing and lane mapping.

The data vendor requires the client to map its internal shipment origins, destination postal codes, and equipment types to the vendor's standard geographic hierarchy.

Vendors run automated outlier filters, but they do not investigate whether a customer's low-rate outliers reflect dedicated fleet backhauls or non-standard freight.

Shippers must independently clean historical freight audit records to extract actionable lane benchmarks.

This precondition dynamic matches logistics automation preconditions are data and contract alignment.

What is claimed, and what is corroborated

  • Vendor claims: Vendors claim 5% to 12% freight procurement savings through market intelligence and rate transparency.
  • Regulatory dockets: Federal Maritime Commission (FMC) fact-finding hearings on maritime container indices documented that public spot indices reflect volatile broker markups during peak shipping disruptions rather than actual long-term carrier billing.
  • Financial disclosures: Roper Technologies (NYSE: ROP) financial reports confirm DAT generates high software operating margins with strong recurring subscription revenue, demonstrating operational lock-in across North American freight brokerage.
