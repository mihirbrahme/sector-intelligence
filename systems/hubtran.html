In 90 seconds

What it does

Back-office document processing and automated freight bill audit platform for freight brokerages and 3PLs

Where it is used

Truckload Brokerage, Inland liquid-bulk marine / tank barge

What it does against the unit of work

HubTran executes operations against the carrier billing packet, scanned proof of delivery (POD), bill of lading (BOL), lumper receipt, and carrier pay line item.

The software automates the accounts payable workflow for freight intermediaries: - Document Classification: Ingests multi-page PDF email attachments and automatically separates invoices, bills of lading, rate confirmations, and accessorial receipts. - Data Extraction: Uses optical character recognition (OCR) and machine-learning models to extract load numbers, invoice amounts, carrier remit-to addresses, and line-item charges. - Three-Way Document Matching: Matches carrier billing packets against active load records in the broker's TMS. - Exception Routing: Flags billing discrepancies (unapproved detention, missing signed delivery receipts, rate mismatches) directly to carrier pay coordinators for exception resolution.

Models and sub-sectors

  • Operating models: Model 1 (Truckload Brokerage) and shipper / BCO in-house transportation management (3PL) — a buyer type, not one of the 13 operating models.
  • Sub-sectors: Non-asset freight brokers, freight forwarders, and managed transportation service providers.
  • Commercial model: Transaction-based SaaS pricing per processed freight invoice ($0.50 to $1.50 per invoice packet), with setup fees.

Market position and customer profile

Prior to its acquisition, HubTran was the undisputed pioneer and market leader in document-processing automation for North American freight brokers.

  • Market footprint: Processed millions of freight invoices monthly across more than 300 brokerage customers.
  • Customer profile: Mid-market freight brokerages and enterprise 3PLs managing 5,000 to over 100,000 loads annually.
  • Strategic acquisition: Acquired by Triumph Financial in 2021 to provide the OCR and data ingestion foundation for the TriumphPay carrier payments network.

Where it is weak

HubTran's automation rates degrade when documents deviate from clean electronic formats: 1. Handwritten and degraded documents: Blurry smartphone photos of bills of lading, handwritten lumper slips, and faded carbon copies fail OCR extraction, dropping into manual review queues. 2. Accessorial reconciliation friction: When carriers invoice for detention or driver layover without prior TMS authorization, HubTran cannot auto-approve the invoice, generating clerk backlog. 3. TMS master data dependency: If the broker's TMS contains inconsistent carrier profiles or inaccurate load numbers, HubTran's matching engine fails to link documents to open payables.

Data model and integration

  • Architecture: Multi-tenant cloud application with browser-based exception processing queues.
  • Machine learning pipeline: Trained on hundreds of millions of historical transportation documents to recognize diverse carrier invoice layouts and bill-of-lading templates.
  • TMS Integrations: Pre-built API connectors to major brokerage management platforms: McLeod PowerBroker, Trimble TMW, MercuryGate, and custom TMS databases.

Ownership and consolidation

  • Founded in 2014 in Chicago, Illinois by Matt Bernstein.
  • Acquired in May 2021 by Triumph Financial, Inc. (NASDAQ: TFIN) for $204 million in cash.
  • Fully integrated into Triumph Financial's payments segment, powering the document capture layer for TriumphPay.

What tends to go wrong

HubTran automates standard packets in seconds, cutting AP personnel cost per load. - billing exception leakage: By verifying that carrier lumper receipts and detention claims match what the broker billed the shipper, HubTran prevents the broker from paying unrecovered carrier accessorials. - purchased-transportation accrual failure: Accelerates the recognition of carrier payables and catches discrepancies between booked rate confirmations and actual billed amounts, preventing aged unvouchered accruals.

What is claimed, and what is corroborated

  • Vendor claims: HubTran claimed a 400% increase in AP clerk productivity, 95% data extraction accuracy, and instant document turnaround.
  • Audited corporate records: Triumph Financial SEC filings for the HubTran acquisition confirmed annual recurring revenue of approximately $12 million at the time of purchase, backed by high renewal rates among top-100 North American freight brokerages.
