In 90 seconds

What it does

Venture-backed real-time transportation visibility platform operating a global multimodal carrier network for shipment tracking and arrival prediction

Where it is used

Truckload Brokerage, Freight Forwarding / NVOCC, Inland liquid-bulk marine / tank barge

What it does against the unit of work

project44 processes the carrier telematics ping, ELD location record, ocean container milestone, and predictive arrival estimate (ETA).

Its flagship platform, Movement, aggregates data feeds across road freight, ocean container shipping, air cargo, and rail networks.

The platform connects to enterprise TMS systems (SAP, Oracle, Blue Yonder, MercuryGate). It ingests active shipment orders and links them to assigned motor carriers and ocean container numbers.

The engine normalises disparate carrier telematics feeds, applies machine learning models to calculate destination arrival times, and issues automated transit exception alerts.

Work it carries

  • Event-driven visibility data pipeline: Ingesting, cleaning, and standardising carrier telematics and container milestone feeds.
  • Detention and demurrage dispute recovery: Logging objective facility arrival, gate-in, and gate-out timestamps.
  • Exception triage and resolution workflow: Flagging delayed shipments to customer service teams before arrival failure.

Models and geographies

  • Operating models: Model 1 (Brokerage), Model 2 (Forwarding), shipper / BCO in-house transportation management — a buyer type, not one of the 13 operating models.
  • Geographies: Global carrier coverage across North America, Europe, Latin America, and Asia-Pacific container lanes.
  • Commercial model: Enterprise subscription SaaS contracts based on annual tracked shipment volume bands. Contracts require multi-year enterprise commitments with substantial annual licensing minimums.

Where it is weak

Platform effectiveness is constrained by carrier network compliance. While integration is automated for major asset carriers, small motor carriers and spot owner-operators frequently fail to connect.

Without aggressive shipper contract enforcement, road freight tracking coverage regularly stalls at 50% to 65%. Gaps force staff back to manual tracking calls.

Geofencing configuration remains an ongoing failure point. Inaccurate customer distribution center coordinates generate false dwell time records and trigger excessive delay notifications.

Venture capital contraction post-2022 forced headcount rationalisation and tighter operational expenditure, shifting focus from aggressive land-grab expansion to operational profitability.

This operational ceiling is analyzed in passive visibility and control towers and visibility platforms fail at carrier data capture.

Who owns the preparation

project44 refuses manual carrier compliance chasing and customer facility geofence auditing.

The vendor provides automated carrier invitation workflows and API documentation. It does not deploy personnel to call thousands of non-compliant independent owner-operators.

project44 assumes the customer possesses accurate polygon geofences for all physical warehouse docks, rail heads, and customer receiving yards.

Shippers must either commit internal transportation coordinators or contract external BPO services to manage carrier compliance and maintain geofence boundaries.

This reality reflects logistics automation preconditions are data and contract alignment.

Who it displaces

  • Global Enterprise Shippers: The leading visibility provider for Fortune 500 multinationals (including Unilever, Amazon, Lenovo, and Mondelēz).
  • North American Freight Brokers: Integrated into major freight brokerage TMS platforms to automate load tracking.
  • Mid-Market Shippers: Adoption is tempered by enterprise pricing thresholds and high carrier onboarding effort.

What is claimed, and what is corroborated

  • Vendor claims: Marketing claims 80% reduction in manual tracking calls, carrier connectivity across 170+ countries, and 95%+ tracking coverage.
  • Gartner research: Ranked as a Leader in the Gartner Magic Quadrant for Real-Time Transportation Visibility Platforms, commended for global data scale while noting enterprise implementation friction in spot carrier onboarding.
  • Financial reporting: Venture disclosures (PitchBook, Wall Street Journal) report over $800M in cumulative capital raised, followed by post-2022 corporate restructuring to achieve cash-flow positive operations amid tighter enterprise software budgets.
