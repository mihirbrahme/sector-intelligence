In 90 seconds

What it does

Software and sensor platforms that aggregate carrier telematics, port milestones, and container events to generate shipment milestone tracking and predictive arrival estimates

Where it is used

Truckload Brokerage, Freight Forwarding / NVOCC, Inland liquid-bulk marine / tank barge

What it does against the unit of work

Real-Time Transportation Visibility Platforms (RTTVPs) process the carrier telematics ping, ELD location update, ocean vessel AIS event, and geofence timestamp.

The software ingests raw location feeds from electronic logging devices (ELD), mobile driver apps, ocean EDI 314 messages, and railway CLM records.

The core engine matches tracking feeds to active TMS load records. It applies predictive algorithms to estimate destination arrival times and flags transit delay exceptions.

Tive combines software with physical cellular and GPS sensor hardware. These single-use or multi-use trackers log location, cargo temperature, shock, and humidity for pharmaceutical and perishables freight.

Primary enterprise vendors include project44, FourKites, Shippeo, and Tive.

Work it carries

  • Event-driven visibility data pipeline: Ingesting, filtering, and standardising raw carrier telematics data.
  • Detention and demurrage dispute recovery: Providing objective facility arrival and departure timestamps.
  • Exception triage and resolution workflow: Routing predicted late deliveries to customer service teams.

Models and geographies

  • Operating models: Model 1 (Brokerage), Model 2 (Forwarding), shipper / BCO in-house transportation management — a buyer type, not one of the 13 operating models.
  • Geographies:
  • Global & North America: project44 and FourKites dominate enterprise shipper tracking programs across North America and ocean freight.
  • Europe: Shippeo is the leading platform across European road freight networks due to integrations with European telematics hardware.
  • Multimodal Sensor: Tive operates globally across cold-chain air, ocean, and road shipments.
  • Commercial model: Enterprise subscription SaaS priced on annual tracked load bands or container moves. Hardware vendors (Tive) charge tracker purchase or lease fees alongside per-shipment data connectivity charges.

Where it is weak

Implementations stall on carrier network compliance. While platforms possess pre-built API connectors to major trucking carriers, spot carriers and small fleets frequently fail to connect.

Without strict commercial mandates, road freight tracking compliance stalls between 40% and 60%. Unmonitored loads force staff back to manual phone calls.

Geofencing accuracy is a severe operational weak point. If customer facility master data contains inaccurate coordinates, circular geofences trigger false dwell alerts.

Massive volumes of false ETA alerts create operator fatigue. Dispatchers begin ignoring platform notifications.

These failures are analyzed in passive visibility and control towers and visibility platforms fail at carrier data capture.

Who owns the preparation

Visibility vendors refuse carrier onboarding enforcement and customer facility master data cleansing.

Software vendors provide automated onboarding portals. They do not assign operational personnel to call thousands of independent owner-operators who refuse ELD tracking consent.

Vendors assume the customer possesses precise polygon geofences for all distribution centers, ocean terminals, and customer delivery sites.

Shippers must either assign internal transportation staff or hire third-party BPO providers to pursue carrier compliance and refine geofence coordinates.

Software cannot overcome absent carrier compliance, as established in logistics automation preconditions are data and contract alignment.

What is claimed, and what is corroborated

  • Vendor claims: Platform marketing claims 95% real-time tracking coverage, 50% dwell time reduction, and 80% fewer track-and-trace phone calls.
  • Gartner research: Gartner Magic Quadrant for Real-Time Transportation Visibility Platforms notes that customer satisfaction scores correlate directly with carrier network coverage rather than analytical software features.
  • Supply chain surveys: Independent shipper benchmarking shows that unassisted carrier tracking compliance rarely exceeds 65% in spot-heavy truckload freight without financial carrier penalties.
