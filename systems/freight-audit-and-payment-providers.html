What it does

Service providers and software vendors that validate carrier freight invoices against contract tariffs, reconcile shipping documents, and execute disbursement

Where it is used

Inland liquid-bulk marine / tank barge

What it does against the unit of work

Freight Audit and Payment (FAP) providers process the carrier freight invoice, bill of lading (BOL), delivery receipt (POD), and remittance disbursement.

The provider ingests carrier bills through EDI 210, APIs, and document parsing engines.

The audit engine executes a three-way match between the carrier invoice, the client's rated shipment record, and the proof of delivery.

The engine audits lane rates, fuel surcharge tables, currency exchange conversions, and accessorial fees against executed transportation contracts.

Discrepancies trigger automated adjustments, short-pays, or rejection workflows. Approved invoices are scheduled for consolidated disbursement to carriers.

The sector divides into two core operating models:

  • Bank-chartered disbursement institutions: Cass Information Systems, U.S. Bank Corporate Payment Systems. These institutions combine audit software with FDIC-regulated banking charters, processing tens of billions in carrier payments.

  • Specialist audit and technology platforms: Trax Technologies, enVista, Loop Logistics. These providers focus on complex global multimodal audit, AI invoice extraction, and supply chain network analytics.

Work it carries

  • Freight audit and payment automation: Systematic invoice line validation and three-way document reconciliation.
  • Detention and demurrage dispute recovery: Verifying detention invoice lines against facility gate logs.
  • Document processing and customs entry automation: Converting unstructured PDF freight bills into machine-readable data.

Models and geographies

  • Operating models: shipper / BCO in-house transportation management — a buyer type, not one of the 13 operating models; secondarily managed transportation 3PLs managing freight spend.
  • Geographies:
  • North America: Cass Information Systems and U.S. Bank lead domestic truckload, LTL, and rail freight audit.
  • Global & Multimodal: Trax Technologies and enVista specialise in cross-border ocean, air, and European freight with complex value-added tax (VAT) handling.
  • Modern Omnichannel: Loop Logistics targets venture-backed and omnichannel consumer brands with complex parcel and freight contracts.
  • Commercial model: Transaction processing fees ($0.50 to $2.00 per processed freight bill), percentage of recovered overcharges (recovery fee or contingency of 15% to 30%), or enterprise subscription SaaS. Bank-chartered processors also generate net interest margin on freight payment float.

Where it is weak

FAP implementations stall when shipper transportation contracts are poorly documented.

If carrier agreements rely on informal rate emails or unratified accessorial schedules, the audit engine rejects up to 25% of invoices.

Massive exception queues overwhelm the shipper's internal transportation team. When staff fail to approve disputed lines, carriers place shipper freight on credit hold.

Global rollouts stall on international tax and currency validation. Local statutory VAT regulations across Europe and APAC require country-specific invoicing logic that generic audit rules cannot handle.

This breakdown is documented in contractual alignment and exception resolution requires commercial authority not workflow software.

Who owns the preparation

Freight audit providers refuse contract negotiation and rate schedule digitisation.

The vendor requires the shipper to deliver executed carrier rate sheets, fuel index schedules, and signed contract amendments in structured formats.

Vendors do not arbitrate commercial disputes between the shipper and its carriers. They identify variance and route the exception back to the client.

Shippers must either dedicate internal logistics analysts or hire specialized consultancies to standardize carrier master agreements before automated audit succeeds.

This dynamic illustrates logistics automation preconditions are data and contract alignment.

What is claimed, and what is corroborated

  • Vendor claims: Providers claim 2% to 6% direct freight spend reduction and 98% straight-through invoice processing.
  • Audited operator disclosures: Cass Information Systems (NASDAQ: CASS) SEC filings report annual freight spend processing of approximately $40 billion, confirming deep market concentration among enterprise industrial shippers.
  • Industry benchmarking: Peer data indicates that straight-through processing rates for multimodal shippers average 70% to 80%, with the remainder requiring manual dispute intervention due to ambiguous accessorial terms.
