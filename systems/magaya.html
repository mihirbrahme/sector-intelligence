In 90 seconds

What it does

All-in-one logistics execution platform combining freight forwarding, customs clearance, and warehouse management for mid-market operators

Where it is used

Freight Forwarding / NVOCC, Contract Logistics / 3PL Warehousing

What it does against the unit of work

Magaya executes operations against the international shipment file, warehouse cargo receipt, freight dimension record, bill of lading, and customs declaration.

The platform provides a unified operational database spanning forwarding and warehousing: - Freight Forwarding: Produces master and house ocean bills of lading (B/L), air waybills (AWB), and cargo manifests. - Bonded Warehousing & CFS: Manages cargo receiving, dimensioning, barcode labeling, pallet storage, and container freight station (CFS) consolidation. - Customs Brokerage: Direct electronic filing with US Customs and Border Protection (CBP ACE) through its integrated customs engine. - Rate Management: Ingests carrier ocean and air contracts, providing spot quoting and margin markup calculations.

Models and sub-sectors

  • Operating models: Model 2 (Freight Forwarding / NVOCC) and Model 3 (Contract Logistics / Warehousing).
  • Sub-sectors: Mid-market air and ocean freight forwarders, container consolidators (NVOCCs), customs brokers, and cross-border logistics operators.
  • Geographic stronghold: Dominant in the South Florida / Miami air and ocean trade gateway, Latin American corridors, and the Caribbean.
  • Commercial model: Subscription SaaS model based on user seats and transaction volume tiers, with implementation and training fees.

Market position and customer profile

Magaya is the primary mid-market alternative to CargoWise for independent forwarders operating across the Americas.

  • Market position: Installed across more than 2,000 logistics companies globally.
  • Customer profile: Mid-market freight forwarders and bonded warehouse operators generating between $5 million and $150 million in gross revenue.
  • Sweet spot: Forwarders operating physical cross-dock or bonded warehouse facilities at air/ocean gateways, where forwarding and warehouse inventory must share a single database.

Where it is weak

Magaya encounters structural limits when competing against Tier 1 enterprise platforms: 1. Limited global multi-branch consolidation: Magaya struggles with the multi-entity, multi-currency corporate consolidation required by global forwarders with hundreds of branches across Asia and Europe. 2. Custom reporting rigidity: Advanced data extraction and business intelligence reporting require complex custom query setups. 3. Advanced warehouse automation limits: The integrated WMS is engineered for pallet and carton cross-docking; it cannot direct complex automated distribution centers (AS/RS, autonomous mobile robots) like Manhattan Active WM. 4. Implementation stalls on tariff digitization: Deployments stall when forwarders attempt to load decades of non-standard air and ocean carrier rate contracts into the rate engine.

Data model and integration

  • Architecture: Hosted cloud SaaS infrastructure and hybrid private-cloud deployments accessed via desktop clients and web browsers.
  • Unified data structure: Cargo dimensions, weights, and warehouse locations recorded at initial warehouse receiving automatically populate downstream master bills of lading and customs entries.
  • Integrations: Direct connections to ocean carrier portals (INTTRA, CargoSmart), air cargo networks (Champ, GLN), and US CBP ACE.
  • APIs: Provides RESTful APIs and webhooks for integration with external customer portals, eCommerce platforms (Shopify), and accounting systems.

Ownership and consolidation

  • Founded in 2001 in Miami, Florida by Jesus David Rodriguez, Gabriel Ruz, and Jesus Cruz.
  • Acquired in 2019 by private equity firm Apax Digital (growth equity arm of Apax Partners).
  • Expanded through acquisitions: Catapult International (ocean freight rate management) in 2020 and ACELynk (cloud customs compliance software) in 2021.

What tends to go wrong

  • billing exception leakage: In air and ocean forwarding, forwarders leak margin when warehouse handling fees, storage dwell, and cargo re-weight charges are not billed to the final invoice. Magaya automatically binds warehouse receiving scales and dimensioners to the shipment file billing tab.
  • forwarding cost pass-through lag on the open shipment file (Forwarding cost pass-through lag on open file): Magaya tracks open shipment files against carrier invoices. If terminal cartage or ocean accessorials arrive late, the system alerts billing coordinators before file closure.
  • forwarding operations establishment fixed against falling file count: Mid-market forwarders suffer margin compression when file counts drop while headcount remains fixed. Magaya's unified document generation reduces clerical touches per file, helping operators maintain file throughput per FTE.
  • unhedged currency exposure inside file margin: In cross-border Latin American forwarding, Magaya tracks buy rates in foreign currencies against USD sales invoices, highlighting FX margin slippage.

What is claimed, and what is corroborated

  • Vendor claims: Magaya claims to automate 80% of logistics workflows, provide total end-to-end supply chain control, and deliver 10x ROI.
  • Trade press and market analysis: Software reviews in FreightWaves and Logistics Management identify Magaya as the most cost-effective integrated WMS/forwarding solution for Americas-focused forwarders, while confirming that global multinational forwarders consistently graduate to CargoWise.
