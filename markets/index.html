Markets

Geographies covered in the record. Each market changes which models exist, what the regulator controls, which costs sit where, and what evidence can be obtained.

United States & Canada

Integrated USMCA freight corridor: Class 1 rail interchange, 53-ft trailer standard, and cross-border trucking.

  • Canada13 modelsCanada looks like a smaller United States and is not one.
  • United States13 modelsThe United States is the only market in this record where all thirteen operating models exist as commercial businesses with public operators.

United Kingdom

Post-Brexit customs regimes (CDS), inventory-linked ports, driver CPC rules, and Companies House reporting.

  • United Kingdom13 modelsThe UK is a short-haul, import-dominated freight market with a customs border that did not exist before 2021.

Southeast Asia

Archipelagic feeder routes, severe directional container imbalances, and state-owned port concessions.

  • Indonesia13 modelsIndonesia is an archipelagic freight economy spanning over 17,000 islands and 5,000 kilometres.
  • Malaysia13 modelsMalaysia is a bifurcated maritime and manufacturing logistics economy.
  • Singapore13 modelsSingapore is a maritime hub first and a domestic freight market second.
  • Southeast AsiaReference analysis of the Southeast Asian freight and logistics market.
  • Thailand13 modelsThailand is an export manufacturing powerhouse and the logistics crossroad of mainland Southeast Asia.

Australia

Long-distance Performance Based Standards (PBS) road trains, coastal shipping rules, and resource rail.

  • Australia13 modelsAustralia is a distance market with two freight economies.
