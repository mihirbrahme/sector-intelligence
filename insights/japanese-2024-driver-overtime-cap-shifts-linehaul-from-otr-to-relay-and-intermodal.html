Japanese 2024 driver overtime cap shifts linehaul from OTR to relay and intermodal

Japan's April 2024 cap of 960 annual driver overtime hours shortened single-driver linehaul range, compressing trucking margins and shifting volume to intermodal and cross-docks

Claim. Japan's April 2024 cap of 960 annual driver overtime hours shortened single-driver linehaul range, compressing trucking margins and shifting volume to intermodal and cross-docks.

Why. Japan's Work Style Reform Law caps driver overtime at 960 hours per year. That limit prevents a single driver from completing overnight trunk round-trips between Kanto, Kansai, Kyushu, and Hokkaido. Operators must split routes at relay cross-docks, buy partner haulage (gaichuhi), or shift freight to rail containers and coastal ferries. Road trucking margins compress relative to 3PL warehousing and in-plant contracting, where operators bill per order line or shift hour.

What was seen. Across five listed Japanese logistics operators in FY2025 and FY3/2026 ):

  • Hamakyorex (TSE:9037, FY3/2026): Logistics Center 3PL (Model 3) earned ¥13,068m OP on ¥100,937m revenue ). Truck Transport (Model 4/5) earned ¥1,712m OP on ¥54,562m revenue ), a 980 bps spread.
  • AZ-COM MARUWA Holdings (TSE:9090, FY3/2026): Logistics sales rose 10.6% from ¥205,598m to ¥227,377m, while segment OP edged up from ¥11,330m to ¥11,650m. Margin compressed from 5.5% to 5.1% on AZ-COM Network partner subcontracting and cold-chain center start-up costs.
  • Loginet Japan (SPSE:9027, FY3/2026): Consolidated revenue reached ¥78,008m with ¥3,711m OP ). East Japan 3PL and intermodal OP rose from ¥1,638m to ¥1,917m ), offsetting Hokkaido road pressure ).
  • Konoike Transport (TSE:9025, FY3/2026): Domestic Logistics (Model 3/4 warehousing and trucking) OP fell from ¥3,807m to ¥3,448m on ¥56,513m sales ). Conversely, in-plant Integrated Solutions grew OP from ¥20,589m to ¥23,864m on ¥231,985m sales ).
  • K.R.S. Corporation (TSE:9369, FY2025): Joint Logistics (Kyodo Butsuryu) earned ¥2,964m OP on ¥137,082m revenue ). Dedicated Logistics earned ¥1,446m OP on ¥39,902m revenue ), constrained by KZN partner trucking subcontracting costs.

What it changes. Separate Japanese logistics filers by how they absorb the 960-hour cap. Treat standalone road haulage (Truck Transport / Domestic Logistics) as structurally margin-capped unless paired with shipper rate surcharges, automated relay cross-docking, or Model 7 rail/ferry modal shift.
