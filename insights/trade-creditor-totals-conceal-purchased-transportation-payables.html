Trade creditor totals conceal purchased transportation payables

In UK statutory filed accounts, trade creditors are bundled into aggregate short-term liabilities, preventing external calculation of days payable outstanding or supplier terms.

Core takeaway

In UK statutory filed accounts, trade creditors are bundled into aggregate short-term liabilities, preventing external calculation of days payable outstanding or supplier terms.

Operating models affected

Freight Forwarding / NVOCC

Where observed

The trap

Assessing working capital health in freight forwarding (operating model 2) requires evaluating the cash conversion cycle: the spread between customer receivable collections and carrier payables (Days Payable Outstanding, or DPO).

Under UK Companies Act reporting formats (FRS 102 Section 1A and standard medium-company formats), the balance sheet discloses a single summary line: Creditors: amounts falling due within one year.

This aggregate line consolidates: - Pure trade payables owed to ocean, air, and road carriers. - Accruals and deferred income. - Corporation tax and payroll liabilities (PAYE/NI). - Customs duty and VAT payable to tax authorities. - Intercompany group balances and short-term director loans.

Computing DPO by dividing this aggregate creditor line by cost of sales produces an arbitrary ratio. The buy-side half of the working-capital cycle described in sector operating models cannot be observed from statutory filed accounts.

Where it was observed

While receivables could be partially isolated, trade creditors could not be separated from tax and accruals in medium filings. DPO and cash conversion cycle metrics could not be computed for any forwarder in the sample.

How to spot it

Never publish a DPO calculation or cash conversion cycle for UK private forwarders based on the top-level balance-sheet creditor figure.

Examine the creditors note to verify whether pure trade payables are broken out separately. If the note does not segregate trade creditors, mark DPO as uncomputable in the metric dictionary rather than computing a contaminated proxy.
