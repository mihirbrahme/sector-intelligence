Operating models affected

Inland liquid-bulk marine / tank barge

Where observed

kirby

Claim. In inland and coastal marine the sellable unit is a class of interchangeable vessel, not a named vessel. Per-asset profitability is not computable — including inside the company — so any proposition resting on per-vessel economics asks for a number that does not exist.

Why. Customers buy a capability, not a hull. Vessels within a class share capacity, hull type and product suitability, and are nominated to jobs day by day on geographic position. Cash flows cannot be traced to an individual asset, so impairment testing is performed at class level too. That is the disclosure which proves the point: impairment grouping follows the lowest level of identifiable independent cash flows.

What was seen. Kirby Corporation FY2025 Form 10-K, filed 2026-02-17, grade ``: "Recoverability on marine transportation assets is assessed based on vessel classes, not on individual assets, because identifiable cash flows for individual marine transportation assets are not available... Because customers generally do not specify which particular vessel is used, prices are quoted based on vessel classes not individual assets. Nominations of vessels for specific jobs are determined on a day by day basis and are a function of the equipment class required and the geographic position of vessels within that class...

Kirby operated 1,105 inland tank barges at FY2025 — 851 petrochemical and refined products, 157 black oil, 87 pressure, 10 refrigerated anhydrous ammonia. Those four groupings are the pricing units.

What it changes. Analyse capacity as class-days, not asset-days: how many vessels of a class, how many days available, where they sit. It also bounds asset-level propositions: per-vessel profit and loss, per-asset return, digital twins keyed to individual hulls. The revenue side of that arithmetic is not attributable. What is attributable at asset level is cost — fuel, crew, maintenance and yard time. Build on the cost side, and read revenue at class level.
