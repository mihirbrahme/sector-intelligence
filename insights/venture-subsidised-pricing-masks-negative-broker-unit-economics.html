Venture-subsidised pricing masks negative broker unit economics

Venture-funded rate discounting and carrier pay premiums create an illusion of algorithmic operational efficiency that collapses the moment balance-sheet capital stops absorbing the gross-to-net spread deficit.

Core takeaway

Venture-funded rate discounting and carrier pay premiums create an illusion of algorithmic operational efficiency that collapses the moment balance-sheet capital stops absorbing the gross-to-net spread deficit.

Operating models affected

Truckload Brokerage

Where observed

05-solutions/failure history/digital-freight-brokerage-matching-algorithms

Why it is true

In truckload brokerage, volume growth is not proof of operational efficiency or technological advantage.

Freight shippers select brokers primarily on rate and tender acceptance. Truckload carriers choose loads primarily on linehaul yield and payment velocity.

A venture-backed broker can generate rapid volume growth and high digital engagement simply by pricing shipper bids below prevailing spot rates while paying carriers above-market linehaul rates.

The resulting transaction volume appears to demonstrate automated marketplace liquidity and network density.

In reality, the growth is bought by burning investor equity to finance a negative gross spread (purchased transportation exceeding gross revenue).

Because the underlying unit of work—the load—remains burdened by manual exception handling (quote-to-book manual handoff), personnel cost per load never scales down to software levels.

When external capital drys up and the company is forced to price for positive spreads, volume evaporates and the business model fails.

What was seen

Documented in digital freight brokerage matching algorithms.

Convoy raised over $900 million and grew rapidly by offering guaranteed tender acceptance and below-market rates to enterprise shippers.

When freight markets softened and venture capital contracted in 2022–2023, the platform could not operate profitably on true broker spreads and collapsed into asset liquidation.

Similarly, Uber Technologies Form 10-K disclosures revealed cumulative Adjusted EBITDA losses of $370 million across 2022–2023 in its Freight segment despite hundreds of millions in capital allocation.

What it changes

Never evaluate a logistics technology platform's operational efficiency by its revenue growth or load volume during capital-expansion phases.

Always demand unit-economics proof: 1. Examine adjusted gross profit (net revenue) per load after deducting third-party purchased transportation. 2. Verify SG&A expense per load against traditional brokerage benchmarks (loads per rep per day). 3. If rapid load growth coincides with continuous operating cash burn, diagnose venture-subsidised rate dumping rather than algorithmic automation.
