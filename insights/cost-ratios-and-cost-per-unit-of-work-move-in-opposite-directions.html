Cost ratios and cost per unit of work move in opposite directions

A cost line rising as a percentage of revenue tells you nothing about whether the work got more expensive. Where volume is disclosed, compute cost per unit of work before concluding anything.

Core takeaway

A cost line rising as a percentage of revenue tells you nothing about whether the work got more expensive. Where volume is disclosed, compute cost per unit of work before concluding anything.

Operating models affected

Protected liner shipping / Jones Act non-contiguous trades

Where observed

matson

Claim. A cost line rising as a percentage of revenue tells you nothing about whether the work got more expensive. Where volume is disclosed, compute cost per unit of work before concluding anything. The two measures routinely point at different lines, and the ratio points at the wrong one.

Why. A ratio to revenue has revenue in the denominator, so it moves with price, mix and volume. Cost per unit of work has the work in the denominator, so it moves only with what the work costs. In a falling-volume, flat-price year the ratio deteriorates on every fixed line at once. That makes the largest overhead line look like the problem, regardless of whether it is.

What was seen. Matson, Inc. FY2025 against FY2024, computed from the disclosed segment cost table and FEU volume table. Volume fell 2.6%, from 401,100 to 390,500 FEU:

Line 2025 / FEU 2024 / FEU Change
General and administrative $753 $757 −0.5%
Vessel operating expense $1,521 $1,522 −0.1%
Operating overhead $843 $820 +2.7%
Direct cargo expense $2,559 $2,444 +4.7%
— of which the terminal JV $875 $800 +9.4%

Read as a ratio, SG&A had risen from 5.8% to 8.5% of revenue since FY2021 and looked like the problem. Per unit of work it was flat. The line that deteriorated was terminal handling, and inside it the joint-venture terminal operator.

What it changes. Where volume is disclosed — FEU, loads, shipment files, order lines, box moves — the unit-cost calculation is one division away. Run it on every material cost line before sizing a value pool on any of them. Where volume is not disclosed, say the pool cannot be sized from outside, rather than substituting an estimated addressable share of a ratio.
