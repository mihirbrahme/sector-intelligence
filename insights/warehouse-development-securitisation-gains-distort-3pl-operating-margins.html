Warehouse development securitisation gains distort 3PL operating margins

In Asian 3PLs that develop or lease property, real estate gains and rents sit inside consolidated operating profit, overstating 3PL margins two- to three-fold

Claim. In Asian 3PLs that develop or lease property, real estate gains and rents sit inside consolidated operating profit, overstating 3PL margins two- to three-fold.

Why. Under Japanese GAAP, merchant warehouse developers and legacy port-warehouse groups classify property leasing and trust-beneficiary securitisation sales as operating revenue. Property leasing and development sales carry 30% to 50% operating margins, whereas Model 3 contract logistics and Model 4 road transport earn 2.5% to 8%.

What was seen. Across five Tokyo Stock Exchange filers ):

  • SBS Holdings (TSE:2384, FY2025): Logistics earned ¥11,888m OP on ¥460,233m sales ). Property Management earned ¥9,142m OP on ¥19,331m sales ), or 42.9% of group ¥21,295m OP.
  • Shibusawa Logistics (TSE:9304, FY3/2026): Logistics earned ¥3,663m OP on ¥73,968m revenue ). Real Estate earned ¥3,134m OP on ¥6,146m revenue ).
  • Mitsui-Soko Holdings (TSE:9302, FY3/2026): Real Estate earned ¥3,661m OP on ¥8,631m revenue ). Logistics earned ¥24,463m OP on ¥291,963m revenue ).
  • Yasuda Logistics (TSE:9324, FY3/2026): Logistics earned ¥5,342m OP on ¥74,167m external revenue ). Real Estate earned ¥2,026m OP on ¥6,480m total segment revenue ).
  • Iino Kaiun Kaisha (TSE:9119, FY3/2026): Real Estate earned ¥4,350m OP on ¥14,180m revenue ). That supplied 32.4% of group ¥13,439m operating profit alongside maritime shipping.

What it changes. Never quote consolidated operating margin for a Japanese or Asian warehouse group. Strip the Property Management or Real Estate segment from both numerator and denominator before benchmarking Model 3 contract logistics performance. Where a 3PL recycles self-developed warehouses into REITs, treat securitisation gains as lumpy capital-recycling income rather than recurring warehouse throughput profit.
