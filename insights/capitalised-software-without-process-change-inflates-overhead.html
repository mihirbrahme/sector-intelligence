Capitalised software without process change inflates overhead

Capitalising internal software development inflates fixed administrative overhead and amortisation when operational exception handling and data entry remain manual.

In 90 seconds

Core takeaway

Capitalising internal software development inflates fixed administrative overhead and amortisation when operational exception handling and data entry remain manual.

Operating models affected

Freight Forwarding / NVOCC

Where observed

pro-carrier, cargo-overseas

The trap

Freight forwarders frequently develop internal operating software. Under accounting standards (FRS 102 / IAS 38), software build costs are capitalised as intangible assets rather than expensed.

When platforms automate only the database of record without eliminating manual touchpoints, labour is not displaced. Operators continue manually re-keying carrier updates and chasing document exceptions.

When software goes live, newly recognised amortisation hits the P&L on top of an unchanged operations payroll. Administrative overhead per unit of gross profit expands, reversing intended productivity gains.

Where it was observed

Documented in mechanism workings uk forwarders §FF-D05: - At Pro Carrier Limited, capitalised internal software additions totaled £1.17m (FY23) and £1.40m (FY24), reaching a £4.89m net book value. Annual amortisation rose from £493k to £651k. Administrative expenses divided by gross profit rose from 89.4% to 96.8%, and staff cost per gross profit climbed from 50.0% to 54.2%. - At Cargo Overseas Limited, directors repeated verbatim across FY24 and FY25 reports that "overhead has increased... with further investment in digital technology," while staff costs per gross profit rose from 36.2% to 41.2%.

How to spot it

Inspect intangible asset notes for capitalised software additions and amortisation schedules.

Compare software growth against administrative expenses as % of gross profit and staff costs in operating model 2. If IT investment expands while administrative burden rises, evaluate whether the company suffers from quote-to-book manual handoff quote-to-book manual handoff.
