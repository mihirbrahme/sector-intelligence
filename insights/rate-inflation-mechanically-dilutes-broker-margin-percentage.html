Rate inflation mechanically dilutes broker margin percentage

Rapid freight linehaul inflation mechanically compresses a broker's gross margin percentage even while gross profit per load and absolute earnings expand.

Core takeaway

Rapid freight linehaul inflation mechanically compresses a broker's gross margin percentage even while gross profit per load and absolute earnings expand.

Operating models affected

Truckload Brokerage

Where observed

rxo, ch-robinson

The trap

In truckload brokerage (operating model 1), standard analysis tracks gross margin percentage (adjusted gross profit divided by gross revenue).

When freight capacity tightens and spot buy rates rise, brokers pass higher costs to shippers.

On a load where gross revenue rises from $2,000 to $2,500 due to rate inflation, an unchanged dollar spread of $300 drops in margin percentage from 15.0% to 12.0%. If the broker expands its dollar spread to $330 (+10%), the margin percentage still contracts to 13.2%.

Analysts tracking only margin percentages diagnose procurement failure. In reality, the broker captured higher absolute dollar profit on every unit of work.

Where it was observed

Over three quarters, RXO's brokerage gross margin fell steadily from 11.9% to 11.4% to 10.7%.

Over the exact same period, RXO's gross profit per load posted sequential growth of 11% in Q2 2026 — the fastest expansion in four years. Revenue per load rose 25%, mechanically diluting the percentage while absolute profitability surged.

A parallel trend appeared at C.H. Robinson Worldwide, Inc. in 2026-Q1: linehaul cost per mile outgrew linehaul revenue per mile by 200 basis points, yet adjusted gross profit per mile increased.

How to spot it

Never rely on gross margin percentages to evaluate broker procurement during freight rate inflection cycles.

Track net revenue per load and total adjusted gross profit. If dollar spread per load holds or rises while margin percentage drops, the contraction is rate gross-up arithmetic, not manual carrier sourcing under rate volatility manual carrier sourcing.
