Commuter pass mix shifts decouple passenger rail yield from ridership

In Asian passenger rail, total ridership misleads because regular tickets and airport express supplements yield 80% to 150% more per passenger-kilometre than commuter passes

Unit of workthe passenger-kilometre

In Asian passenger rail, total ridership misleads because regular tickets and airport express supplements yield 80% to 150% more per passenger-kilometre than commuter passes

Operating models affected

Urban & Commuter Passenger Rail and Transit-Oriented Network

Where observed

tokyo-metro, keikyu, odakyu-electric-railway, kyushu-railway, nankai-electric-railway

Claim. In Asian passenger rail, total ridership misleads because regular tickets and airport express supplements yield 80% to 150% more per passenger-kilometre than commuter passes.

Why. Under Japanese MLIT yardstick regulation, work and school commuter passes (teikiken) carry statutory discounts of 35% to over 60% per trip. Regular IC-card tickets (teikigai), reserved-seat express supplements, and airport fares carry full tariffs and near-100% incremental margin. Shifting from commuter passes to regular tickets and airport trips expands farebox yield per passenger-kilometre even when boardings plateau.

What was seen. Across five listed Japanese Model 19 operators in FY3/2025 and FY3/2026 ):

  • Tokyo Metro (TSE:9023): Transportation revenue rose from ¥353,890m (FY3/2024) to ¥386,600m (FY3/2026). Transportation OP reached ¥76,100m ) on central Tokyo non-commuter commercial travel recovery.
  • Keikyu (TSE:9006): Transportation revenue rose to ¥121,591m with ¥18,683m OP ) in FY3/2026. High-yield Haneda Airport Line demand and the October 2023 tariff revision drove the gain.
  • Odakyu Electric Railway (TSE:9007): Transportation OP rose from ¥26,495m ) in FY3/2025 to ¥29,517m on ¥181,261m revenue ) in FY3/2026. Reserved-seat Romancecar express and Hakone inbound tourism drove the margin expansion.
  • Kyushu Railway (TSE:9142): Transportation OP nearly doubled from ¥12,125m ) in FY3/2025 to ¥23,976m on ¥190,668m revenue ) in FY3/2026. Its April 2025 ~15.0% fare and express surcharge hike lifted yields across Shinkansen and commuter lines.
  • Nankai Electric Railway (TSE:9044): Transportation OP rose from ¥13,263m ) in FY3/2025 to ¥14,908m on ¥117,329m revenue ) in FY3/2026. Kansai Airport Rapi:t express and Expo 2025 visitor flows drove the increase.

What it changes. Never forecast Model 19 rail revenue from aggregate passenger boardings alone. Split railway traffic tables into commuter-pass (teikiken), non-commuter (teikigai), and express supplement cohorts, and compute farebox yield per passenger-kilometre for each.
