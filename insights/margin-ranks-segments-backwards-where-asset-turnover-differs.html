Margin ranks segments backwards where asset turnover differs

Claim. In a group running two operating models with materially different asset intensity, ranking the segments on operating margin can invert the ranking on return on the capital each employs. The higher-margin segment is not necessarily the better business. Saying it is inverts the capital-allocation conclusion.

Why. Operating margin has revenue in the denominator; return on capital has the assets in it. Where two segments turn their assets at different rates, the two measures are not proxies for one another. bars putting two operating models' financial performance in one frame at all — this is the arithmetic showing why.

What was seen. Kirby Corporation FY2025 segment disclosure, Form 10-K filed 2026-02-17, grade ``; ratios:

Marine Transportation Distribution & Services
Revenue $1,935.3m $1,428.7m
Operating income $374.5m $130.8m
Operating margin 19.3% 9.2%
Segment assets $4,705.7m $1,111.5m
Asset turnover 0.41x 1.29x
Return on segment assets 8.0% 11.8%

On margin, marine looks like the business carrying the group and distribution the drag. On return on the assets each uses, the ranking reverses. A three-fold difference in asset turnover does all of the work.

The same group's SG&A shows the companion trap: 7.5% of revenue in marine, 14.1% in distribution, 10.7% consolidated. The consolidated figure describes neither business and matches no peer set, so a claim about cost efficiency built on it is built on a mix artefact.

What it changes. Compute segment asset turnover first, before quoting any consolidated ratio or ranking segments on margin. Where the turnovers differ by more than roughly two-fold, treat the group as two companies for every purpose: peer set, benchmark, working capital, cost efficiency. Where segment assets are not disclosed, say the segment return cannot be computed, rather than substituting the group figure. Matson, Inc. states that segment balance sheet information is not provided to its CODM, so no segment return exists to quote.
