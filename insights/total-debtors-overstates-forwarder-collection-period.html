Total debtors overstates forwarder collection period

Computing debtor days from total balance-sheet debtors heavily overstates the collection period for customs-active freight forwarders.

In 90 seconds

Core takeaway

Computing debtor days from total balance-sheet debtors heavily overstates the collection period for customs-active freight forwarders.

Operating models affected

Freight Forwarding / NVOCC

Where observed

cargo-overseas

The trap

Standard financial screeners compute debtor days by dividing total balance-sheet debtors by turnover and multiplying by 365.

In UK and statutory accounts, the Debtors line is a consolidated total. It aggregates trade receivables, prepayments, accrued income, intercompany balances, and duty and VAT advanced on behalf of shippers.

For customs-active operators in operating model 2, duty and VAT disbursements are substantial. These disbursements inflate the balance-sheet asset without generating matching revenue in the denominator. The numerator expands while the denominator reflects only forwarding fees and freight charges, corrupting the collection day calculation.

Where it was observed

At Cargo Overseas Limited in FY2024, balance-sheet debtors produced a computed debtor-day figure of 105.9 days.

In the same annual filing, the company's directors reported an actual trade receivable collection period of 55 days as an internal KPI (32 days in FY2023, 55 days in FY2024, 55 days in FY2025). The computed metric overstated actual customer credit terms by nearly 51 days.

Similar distortions appeared at Supreme Freight Services Limited (computed 97 days on ex-duty revenue versus 56 days statutory) and Scan Global Logistics (UK) Ltd (183.5 computed days in FY2023 driven by balance-sheet composition shifts rather than collections distress).

How to spot it

Never rank freight forwarders using standard screener debtor days.

Review the balance sheet notes to isolate pure trade receivables from prepayments, group balances, and tax disbursements. Where companies publish director-stated receivable KPIs, use the stated figure. When reading balance-sheet totals, track directional changes over time rather than absolute day levels, or evaluate billing exception leakage billing exception leakage through interest expense trends.
