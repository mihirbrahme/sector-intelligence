Equity method associates and concession receivables inflate transit net income

In transport groups holding minority stakes in theme parks, ports, or LNG ventures, associate income can exceed transport EBIT, making net margin unusable

Claim. In transport groups holding minority stakes in theme parks, ports, or LNG ventures, associate income can exceed transport EBIT, making net margin unusable.

Why. Under IFRS and Japanese GAAP, equity-accounted income from 20%-to-50% associates sits below operating profit (EBIT) or inside ordinary profit (Keijo Rieki). Zero associate revenue enters the consolidated top line, so dividing net or ordinary profit by operating revenue mismatches numerator and denominator. Concession accounting and associate impairments also swing bottom-line net profit independently of core farebox or vessel cash flows.

What was seen. Across three listed rail and maritime infrastructure groups ):

  • Keisei Electric Railway (TSE:9009, FY3/2026): Reported operating profit of ¥33,974m on ¥332,424m revenue ), while ordinary profit reached ¥63,042m ). Equity-method earnings from its ~21% stake in Tokyo Disney Resort operator Oriental Land (TSE:4661) added ¥29,068m above OP ).
  • Qatar Navigation (Milaha) (QSE:QNNS, FY2025): Reported operating profit before impairments of QAR 669m on QAR 3,327m revenue ), while net profit reached QAR 1,271m ). Associate income from 49%-owned QTerminals and Nakilat LNG charters plus Milaha Capital returns exceeded operating profit by 90.0%.
  • BTS Group Holdings (SET:BTS, FY2025/26): Generated THB 11,861m recurring EBITDA on THB 23,800m operating revenue (MOVE rail THB 9,699m, MIX media THB 4,652m, MATCH property THB 9,449m). However, Pink and Yellow monorail concession ramp-up losses and associate losses drove a THB (1,152)m consolidated net loss (vs. THB 2,324m net profit in FY2024/25).

What it changes. Never screen or value Model 6, Model 12, or Model 19 infrastructure groups on consolidated net margin or P/E multiples. Evaluate the core transport operation strictly on segment EBIT or recurring EBITDA, and value equity-method stakes separately via sum-of-the-parts.
