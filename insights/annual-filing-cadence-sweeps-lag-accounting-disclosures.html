Annual filing cadence sweeps lag accounting disclosures

Monitoring annual filing cadence breaches lags major accounting breakdowns by months, because companies disclose restatements via Form 8-K and Form 12b-25 before annual reports become overdue.

Core takeaway

Monitoring annual filing cadence breaches lags major accounting breakdowns by months, because companies disclose restatements via Form 8-K and Form 12b-25 before annual reports become overdue.

Where observed

hub-group, the source recordsource-register

The distinction

Screening corporate filing calendars detects distress when a public registrant breaches its historical filing month by more than four months.

Cadence detectors suffer from a severe lag problem: they fire only after a company has already gone dark.

Before an annual report is overdue, corporate reporting rules compel explicit disclosures: - Form 8-K Item 4.02 must be filed within four business days of an audit committee restatement decision. - Form 12b-25 (Notification of Late Filing) must be submitted on the statutory due date.

Relying on cadence sweeps means learning about accounting failures months after market participants have priced them.

Where it was observed

Across 134 SEC registrants, Hub Group, Inc. triggered a filing-cadence detector in late March 2026 and appeared 6.3 months overdue by September 2026.

Hub Group had filed its Form 8-K Item 4.02 non-reliance disclosure on 2026-02-05, followed by Form NT 10-K on 2026-03-03. Monitoring Item 4.02 and Form NT surfaced the accounting failure nearly a month before the cadence detector fired.

The 134-registrant sweep that produced the comparison was run under the retired lead-qualification machinery and its record was removed on 2026-09-16; it survives in git history at 08-pipeline/the source recordmissed-filing-sweep. The two Hub Group filing dates above are independently checkable on EDGAR and do not depend on it.

How to spot it

Do not rely on annual calendar sweeps as primary detection triggers across all operating models, as unannounced accounting restatements directly distort operating ratio and violate purchased transportation accrual mechanisms like purchased-transportation accrual failure.

Configure ingestion pipelines to monitor Form 8-K Item 4.02 and Form 12b-25 directly, per source register. Use cadence sweeps as secondary backstops to catch unannounced corporate disappearances.
