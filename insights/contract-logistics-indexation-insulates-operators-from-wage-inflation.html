Contract logistics indexation insulates operators from wage inflation

Contract logistics contracts frequently embed open-book indexation and productivity commitments, insulating operators from wage inflation that degrades asset-based carriage.

Core takeaway

Contract logistics contracts frequently embed open-book indexation and productivity commitments, insulating operators from wage inflation that degrades asset-based carriage.

Operating models affected

Contract Logistics / 3PL Warehousing

Where observed

gxo-logistics

The distinction

In transportation analysis, wage inflation is assumed to degrade operating margins unless offset by general rate increases. Analysts often apply this assumption to contract logistics and warehousing (operating model 3), diagnosing margin compression when labour markets tighten.

This misinterprets the commercial structure of modern contract logistics.

Large pure-play 3PL warehouse operators write multi-year customer agreements under two protected structures: 1. Open-book cost-plus, where facility and labour inflation are directly reimbursed by the customer at a pre-agreed margin markup. 2. Closed-book hybrid contracts containing contractual inflation indexation clauses that trigger automatic price adjustments.

Furthermore, warehouse operators deploy goods-to-person automation, robotics, and machine-learning labour scheduling to meet contractual productivity targets. As a result, direct operating margins remain stable through inflationary environments.

Where it was observed

While freight carriers suffered margin compression from wage pressure, GXO disclosed that its contracts were overwhelmingly open-book cost-plus or indexed.

Backed by automation, GXO held direct operating expenses essentially flat at 85.1% to 85.2% of revenue across 2025-Q4 to 2026-Q2, improving 80 basis points year-on-year in 2026-Q1.

This observation prompted the narrowing of contract vintage margin decay contract vintage margin decay, which now restricts its scope exclusively to contracts lacking indexation and productivity offsets.

How to spot it

Review Form 10-K contract structure disclosures and revenue notes. Check the proportion of open-book versus fixed-price agreements in operating model 3.

Do not assert labour cost inflation failures in the mechanism library without establishing contract indexation terms.
