Sale and leaseback trading gains mask core airline flight losses

Low-cost carriers with large OEM orderbooks book upfront aircraft sale-and-leaseback gains inside operating profit, masking thin flight margins while raising future lease CASK

Unit of workthe Available Seat Mile

In 90 seconds

Core takeaway

Low-cost carriers with large OEM orderbooks book upfront aircraft sale-and-leaseback gains inside operating profit, masking thin flight margins while raising future lease CASK

Operating models affected

Passenger Network Legacy Carrier, Point-to-Point Low-Cost Carrier

Where observed

vietjet-aviation, cebu-air

Claim. Low-cost carriers with large OEM orderbooks book upfront aircraft sale-and-leaseback gains inside operating profit, masking thin flight margins while raising future lease CASK.

Why. Airlines placing bulk Airbus or Boeing orders secure steep launch discounts below market appraisal values. When the carrier sells a newly delivered aircraft to a lessor and leases it back, local accounting standards or OEM compensation structures allow upfront gain recognition. The transaction converts higher USD lease payments over the next 8 to 12 years into current-year operating profit.

What was seen. Observed at two Southeast Asian Model 15 carriers ):

  • VietJet Aviation (HOSE:VJC, FY2025): Reported revenue of VND 82,093bn (+14.2% YoY), PBT of VND 2,630bn, and PAT of VND 2,123bn ). Under Vietnamese Accounting Standards (VAS), VietJet books upfront aircraft sale-and-leaseback (SLB) trading revenue and gross profit inside operating income. Its system-wide fleet expanded from 94 to 135 aircraft while net debt-to-equity rose to 2.25x.
  • Cebu Air (PSE:CEB, FY2025): Reported revenue of PHP 119,900m (+14.3% YoY) and operating income of PHP 11,500m ). Net income jumped 127.8% YoY to PHP 12,300m ), exceeding EBIT. Fleet growth to 100 aircraft combined with Pratt & Whitney PW1100G GTF grounding compensation and spare-engine credits (engine rotable durability deficit).

What it changes. Never evaluate a fast-growing low-cost carrier on consolidated operating or net margin before disaggregating the revenue and other-operating-income footnotes. Strip aircraft trading gross profit and non-recurring OEM grounding credits from EBIT to isolate core flight RASK minus CASK. Capitalise the resulting lease obligations when comparing unit costs against owned-fleet peers.
