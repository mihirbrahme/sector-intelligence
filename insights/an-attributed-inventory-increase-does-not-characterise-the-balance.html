An attributed inventory increase does not characterise the balance

When a filing explains the movement in inventory, that explanation describes the delta, not the stock. Applying it to the whole balance converts a routine working-stock position into an apparent operational failure.

Core takeaway

When a filing explains the movement in inventory, that explanation describes the delta, not the stock. Applying it to the whole balance converts a routine working-stock position into an apparent operational failure.

Operating models affected

Contract Logistics / 3PL Warehousing, Inland liquid-bulk marine / tank barge

Where observed

kirby

Claim. When a filing explains the movement in inventory, that explanation describes the delta, not the stock. Applying it to the whole balance converts a routine working-stock position into an apparent operational failure.

Why. MD&A explains variances. "Inventories increased primarily due to X" is an attribution of the change, and the change is often a small fraction of the balance. The sentence carries no information about the rest, which is ordinary trading stock accumulated over years.

What was seen. Kirby Corporation FY2025 Form 10-K, filed 2026-02-17, grade ``. The cash flow discussion states that the increase in inventories was "due to the impact of higher business activity levels and efforts to manage supply in KDS resulting in the buildup of inventory for projects, mainly due to power generation orders, which are scheduled to be delivered in 2026."

The balances:

FY2025 FY2024 Change
Finished goods $340.7m $328.5m +$12.2m
Work in process $57.3m $65.4m −$8.1m
Total inventories, net $398.0m $393.9m +$4.1m, +1%

Inventory rose 1%. The attribution covers roughly $12m of finished-goods movement, not the $340.7m balance. Against segment cost of sales of $1,045.4m, finished goods is 119 days and total inventory 139 days. For a 62-branch parts and engine distribution network with single-source OEM dependency, that is working stock.

Read the other way, the $340.7m becomes "a third of a billion dollars of finished goods that cannot ship", which is a different and unsupported claim.

What it changes. Whenever a value case rests on an inventory, receivable or payable balance, check whether the filing's explanation attaches to the balance or to the movement. Then compute the movement. Then benchmark the balance against the right peer set. A distributor's inventory days belong beside dealer peers — Finning, Toromont, Wajax, Alta Equipment — not beside a cross-industry working capital median that includes businesses holding no stock at all.
