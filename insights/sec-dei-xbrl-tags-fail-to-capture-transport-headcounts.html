SEC DEI XBRL tags fail to capture transport headcounts

SEC Document and Entity Information (DEI) XBRL taxonomy tags omit employee headcount for transport and logistics filers, who report human capital narratively in Item 1 text.

In 90 seconds

Core takeaway

SEC Document and Entity Information (DEI) XBRL taxonomy tags omit employee headcount for transport and logistics filers, who report human capital narratively in Item 1 text.

Where observed

the source recordsource-register, the source recordfiling-universe-us

The trap

Programmatic financial pipelines querying the SEC EDGAR API attempt to extract full-time employee counts via standard XBRL taxonomy concepts (such as dei/EntityNumberOfEmployees).

In US corporate reporting, tagging the employee headcount tag in the DEI schema is optional for most non-accelerated and commercial filers.

While passenger airlines and retail conglomerates sometimes populate the tag, transport and logistics operators overwhelmingly disclose workforce figures in narrative tables within Form 10-K Item 1 (Human Capital Resources).

Automated pipelines relying exclusively on dei/EntityNumberOfEmployees return null or obsolete figures for transport registrants, leading algorithms to flag public logistics companies as missing staffing data.

Where it was observed

Out of 131 transport and logistics public filers, only 7 companies populated the dei/EntityNumberOfEmployees XBRL tag. Most of those 7 represented legacy, unmaintained data from passenger airlines.

Major transport operators — including truckload carriers, brokers, and logistics intermediaries — returned null, despite disclosing full employee decompositions (such as driver counts, terminal staff, and back-office headcount) in the text of Item 1.

How to spot it

Never rely on XBRL taxonomy tags to extract workforce numbers for US logistics filers across all operating models.

Route headcount extraction to regular-expression parsing or LLM extraction of Form 10-K Item 1 / Item 1A text. Verify extracted figures before calculating productivity metrics in the metric dictionary (such as loads per rep per day or net revenue per employee).
