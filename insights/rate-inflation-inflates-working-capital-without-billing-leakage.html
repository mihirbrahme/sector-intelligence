Rate inflation inflates working capital without billing leakage

Rising freight rates mechanically expand receivables and drain operating cash in brokerage, driving days sales outstanding higher without internal billing or collections failure.

Core takeaway

Rising freight rates mechanically expand receivables and drain operating cash in brokerage, driving days sales outstanding higher without internal billing or collections failure.

Operating models affected

Truckload Brokerage

Where observed

ch-robinson, rxo, knight-swift, landstar

The trap

Brokerage working capital exhibits structural asymmetry. Brokers pay outside motor carriers on short terms (2 to 20 days) but collect from shippers on standard terms (40 to 60 days).

When freight rates rise, gross invoice amounts expand proportionally across both sides. Because receivables are held longer than payables, working capital requirements scale directly with gross revenue.

Cash is absorbed into receivables even if load counts and profitability remain stable.

Analysts evaluating widening days sales outstanding often diagnose billing leakage. In an inflecting rate environment, DSO expansion is the mathematical outcome of financing higher gross load values.

Where it was observed

  • C.H. Robinson Worldwide, Inc. reported operating cash flow falling from $227.1m in 2025-Q2 to $35.9m in 2026-Q2 — a $191.2m cash drain — while adjusted EPS surged 24.8%.
  • RXO, Inc. swung from $22m cash provided in H1 2025 to $3m cash used in H1 2026.
  • Knight-Swift Transportation Holdings Inc. executed a $575.0m accounts receivable securitisation facility to fund the expanding float.
  • Landstar System, Inc. saw working capital contract from $646.7m to $520.5m.

None of these instances involved billing errors or counterparty defaults.

How to spot it

Cross-check rate indices before attributing a rising DSO to billing exception leakage billing exception leakage.

Measure receivables growth against average revenue per load in operating model 1. If receivables expansion tracks gross revenue per load while aged debt buckets remain healthy, the cash drain is macro rate absorption.
