Energy shipping: LNG, crude, product, offshore FPSO/FSO

October is confirmed as the worst month of the Hormuz war by two independent counts, and both likely understate it

2026-10-27 reversed a "cooling" read on Hormuz tanker attacks but could not tell whether four different trackers were counting the same thing. Two of them have now converged on the same month, and that convergence is the stronger basis. October is confirmed as the worst month of the war for tanker attacks, by an institutional count and an independent one together. The International Maritime Organization counted 27 attacks on commercial ships in October as of 2026-10-22.

Asset Carriage / Truckload Fleet

Werner's swing from a loss to a profit is substantially a FirstFleet acquisition story, not a market recovery signal

Werner's Q3 2026 swing from an operating loss to a profit reads like a recovery, but most of the revenue growth behind it was bought, not earned back from the market. The FirstFleet acquisition, closed in January, did the work. Reading this quarter as evidence the freight market turned in Werner's favour overstates what happened. Werner reported Q3 2026 revenue of $950.5 million, up 23% year over year; operating income of $17.0 million against an operating loss of $13.0 million a year earlier; and adjusted diluted EPS of $0.16 against an adjusted loss of $0.03.

Domestic Intermodal

2026-10-27's intermodal-network comparison used the wrong weeks, and the right ones reopen the question it claimed to close

2026-10-27 compared Knight-Swift Transportation Holdings Inc.'s Q3 load growth to AAR's October weekly intermodal figures and read the match as confirmation the company is riding a sector-wide demand wave. The two periods do not overlap. Knight-Swift's fiscal Q3 ran through 2026-09-30; the AAR weeks cited (ending 2026-10-03, 2026-10-10 and 2026-10-17) sit entirely in Q4. The closer comparator is AAR's cumulative year-to-date intermodal count, up 4.6% through week 41 of 2026 — a window running to roughly 2026-10-11 that already includes part of October's acceleration.
