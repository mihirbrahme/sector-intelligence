Asset Carriage / Truckload Fleet

Schneider's Truckload beat looks like Knight-Swift's capacity-discipline signature, but a Dedicated mix shift is the more likely cause

2026-10-27 asked whether a second large carrier would show Knight-Swift's fewer-trucks, higher-yield signature. Schneider's Q3 looks like a match, but the same mix effect 2026-10-28 found inflating Werner's quarter may be doing the same work here, and the record cannot yet tell the two apart. Schneider reported Q3 2026 Truckload segment operating ratio of 93.0%, improved 380 basis points from 96.8% a year earlier, on segment revenue excluding fuel surcharge of $624.8 million, roughly flat year over year. Revenue per truck per week rose 8% to $4,252.

LTL

Saia's record tonnage and widening operating ratio are the volume-driven deleverage Old Dominion's quarter did not show

2026-10-29 flagged Old Dominion's Q3 as price-driven on flat tonnage, against Model 5's usual volume-driven recovery mechanism, and asked whether Saia's own result would show the ordinary pattern instead. It shows neither — Saia's volume surged and its margin still worsened. Saia reported Q3 2026 revenue of $982.5 million, up 17.0% year over year, with LTL tonnage per workday up 8.9% to a company record while LTL shipments per workday rose only 0.8%. Operating income rose just 3.1% to $122.3 million and operating ratio worsened 170 basis points, to 87.6% from 85.9%.

Truckload Brokerage

RXO's brokerage margin reversed for the first time this cycle, and spot mix explains it better than sourcing discipline

RXO's brokerage gross margin turned up for the first time since the record began tracking it, but the disclosed cause is a spot-mix shift, not better carrier buying. Reading this as manual carrier sourcing under rate volatility resolving itself overstates what moved. RXO reported Q3 2026 revenue of $1.87 billion, up 31.7% year over year, and brokerage gross margin of 12.6%. Truckload gross profit per load rose 31% sequentially, the largest gain in the series, which the company attributes to spot mix reaching 51% of volume against 29% a year earlier and 42% last quarter.
