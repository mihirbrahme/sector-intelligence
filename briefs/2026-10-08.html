Asset Carriage / Truckload Fleet

FMCSA's English-proficiency rule is one day from closing comment, and finalising it converts a reversible enforcement policy into binding regulation

The benchmark register already carries this instrument among the rules that "convert a records system into a capacity-removal mechanism." What changes this week is its legal footing, not its existence. FMCSA's proposed rule making English-language proficiency an out-of-service violation — docket FMCSA-2026-0826, RIN 2126-AC99, Federal Register document 2026-16288, published 2026-08-10 in response to a CVSA petition — closes its public comment window on 2026-10-09 or 2026-10-10, sources disagree by a day. The enforcement policy the rule would codify is already producing out-of-service orders in the low thousands, per 2026-10-07's FMCSA-sourced count of 8,953.

Asset Carriage / Truckload Fleet

Wall Street cut truckload estimates for Q3 and Q4 together this week, which reads as a longer downturn being priced in, not a seasonal miss

Cutting two quarters in one note is a different claim than trimming the next one. It says the analyst no longer expects a normal Q4 seasonal bounce to offset a soft Q3. Susquehanna's Bascome Majors downgraded Knight-Swift Transportation Holdings Inc. to Neutral, cut its price target to $43 from $52, and trimmed its Q3 2026 EPS estimate roughly 5%, citing a longer cyclical downturn than previously modelled; Schneider National's Q3 estimate fell roughly 12%, around 14% below prevailing consensus; Werner Enterprises's fell 11%; J.B. Hunt's fell 6%. The benchmark register's own FY2025 reading already puts Model 4 Irregular-Route OTR operating ratio at a 99.8% median, the trough of its five-year series — this note is the estimate-revision evidence that the trough is not expected to lift on schedule.

Truckload Brokerage

2026-10-06 logged CANACAR's estimate of roughly 25,000 B-1 visas revoked from Mexican commercial drivers since the crackdown tightened, with 150–200 in the Mexicali region. 2026-10-07 added an operational count from a Nuevo Laredo delegate — roughly 3,000 trucks a day crossing at Laredo, down from 4,500. This session finds two more figures in the same chain: CANACAR president Augusto Ramos Melo cites roughly 20,000 revocations over April 2025 to April 2026, and a separate Nuevo Laredo CANACAR delegate cites approximately 3,200 drivers region-wide. Four numbers — 25,000, 20,000, 3,200, and the 3,000-trucks-a-day operational count — all trace to CANACAR officials speaking through the same restatement chain, and none is confirmed by CBP, the State Department, or DOT.
