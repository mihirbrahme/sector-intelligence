International container liner and feeder shipping

Suez capacity is returning on the empty leg faster than on the paying leg, and that gap is propping up rates

The Suez return reads as one capacity event only if both directions return together. Asia-to-Europe headhaul share has held near 13-25% through September while Europe-to-Asia backhaul share jumped from 18-26% in August to 25-47% in September. A return concentrated on the empty leg frees ships for repositioning without adding sellable headhaul slots. DAT's August spot crash turns the truckload tightening story into a contract-season bet

Load-to-truck ratios still say capacity is tight. Spot pricing said something different in August. Whether the tightening story holds now depends on the September-October contract season, not on the driver-removal read this record logged on 2026-09-18.
