Ports & Terminals

A fourth deadline extension at LA/Long Beach confirms stalemate over a deal taking shape

2026-11-06 read the serial short extensions in the ILWU Local 26 watchmen dispute as evidence of a genuine standoff, not an approaching deal, and named the test directly: a fourth extension would strengthen that reading. It has arrived, and it is a bigger jump than the first three. The Pacific Maritime Association and Local 26 moved their contract deadline from midnight Pacific on 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-20. That is a two-week jump, not another few days.

International container liner and feeder shipping

The Section 301 China-vessel-fee suspension passed its own deadline with no confirmed extension on the public record

The likely outcome is still that the suspension continues in substance — USTR proposed it, nobody opposed it, and the US and China agreed the extension at leader level in September. But the document that makes that legally true has not been found, and the deadline it was meant to beat has already passed. USTR's proposal, a Federal Register notice of 2026-10-26 under docket USTR-2026-0694, would extend the suspension from 2026-11-10 through 2027-01-09. The comment period closed 2026-10-30 with no reported opposition found this session.

Asset Carriage / Truckload Fleet

Knight-Swift is pricing the 2027 bid season for scarcity, not demand, and funding a Northeast LTL entry from the proceeds

Knight-Swift's own framing of its Q3 recovery, and its refusal to reaffirm a cost target set earlier this year, both point to a carrier betting on tight capacity rather than a cyclical demand rebound — and using that bet to fund entry into a second operating model. Knight-Swift's adjusted truckload operating ratio improved to 89.3% in Q3 2026, which management attributed to tightening capacity rather than freight demand. CEO Adam Miller guided to "low to mid double-digit" contract rate increases for the 2027 bid season, and the company did not reaffirm the sub-90% operating ratio target it set earlier in 2026, while disclosing a planned Northeast LTL expansion for 2027. An 89.3% OR sits far above the Model 4 FY2025 median of 99.8% recorded in the register — a move large enough to verify directly against the 10-Q before relying on it further.
