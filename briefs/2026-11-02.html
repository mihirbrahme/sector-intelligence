LTL

Three large LTL carriers posted three different margin mechanisms this reporting week, so none of them is "the" LTL recovery story

XPO's Q3 2026 LTL margin gain came from tonnage and yield rising together — the releverage signature Model 5's financial signature describes as the normal recovery path. 2026-10-29 found Old Dominion's margin gain running on price alone with flat tonnage. 2026-10-30 found Saia's margin worsening despite record tonnage. Three of the sector's largest public carriers show three distinct signatures in the same reporting week, which argues against reading any one of them as the sector's recovery story.

Asset Carriage / Truckload Fleet

Covenant's Dedicated yield gain is a disclosed contract-exit strategy, not the undisclosed blended mix shift flagged at Werner and Schneider

2026-10-28 and 2026-10-30 flagged Werner's and Schneider's blended Truckload-segment gains as possibly a Dedicated mix shift rather than genuine capacity discipline, with the sub-segment split undisclosed in both cases. Covenant's Q3 shows what that mix shift looks like when a company actually names the mechanism: a deliberate exit from unprofitable Dedicated contracts, not an unexplained blend. Covenant reported Q3 2026 Dedicated revenue per tractor per week of $4,810, up 6.2% year over year, which the company attributed to growth in agricultural-protein-related transportation and its own decision to exit less profitable dedicated contracts. Its Q2 2026 Form 10-Q had already disclosed that roughly 25% of the Dedicated fleet renews within 12 months, and named many of those contracts as its least profitable.

Energy shipping: LNG, crude, product, offshore FPSO/FSO

October's Hormuz attacks are diversifying by method, not just by count, and the method shift is the harder risk to price

2026-10-28 established October as the worst month of the Hormuz war by attack count. The more consequential change this week is not the count but the method. Within three weeks the strait has seen a projectile or mine strike that sank a container ship, and now a small boat fixing explosive charges directly to a tanker's hull. A widening set of attack methods is harder to insure and harder to defend against with naval escort than a rising count of one kind of attack.
