Truckload Brokerage

Montgomery's liability shock is repricing broker insurance capacity, not just broker balance sheets

carrier-selection liability repricing the cost of cheap capacity already logs Montgomery v. Caribe Transport II as its trigger event, citing C.H. Robinson Worldwide, Inc.'s own revised Item 1A warning. What is new this week is where the cost is landing: the price and availability of the liability cover itself, alongside claims reserves. RPS Transportation, an insurance broker specialising in freight risk, told The Insurer on 2026-09-17 that Montgomery is "already creating a capacity issue for freight broker contingent liabilities".

Domestic Intermodal

STB's refusal to summarily kill UP-NS forces the merger fight onto the November merits record

The Surface Transportation Board denied three motions for summary denial of the UP-NS merger application on 2026-09-18. BNSF, CSX and a five-member shipper coalition — led by the American Chemistry Council and the National Industrial Transportation League — filed the motions on 2026-08-06. The board said the denial reflects no view on the merits, and does not bar the same arguments in opening comments, due 2026-11-18. This is a loss for the separate procedural track the same rivals opened on 2026-09-14, when BNSF, CPKC and CSX asked STB to force UP and NS to certify their Hunt/Oliver Wyman analysis final by 2026-09-19, arguing repeated Friday-night amendments show the application's foundation is broken (per the 2026-09-15 and 2026-09-17 briefs).

Ports & Terminals

Near-record LA-Long Beach volume is clearing the berth but backing up on the rail leg, not the quay

Combined Los Angeles-Long Beach volume ran near-record in August, and the terminals cleared it fine by truck. The strain shows up one step downstream, on the rail evacuation leg the terminals do not themselves run. The Port of Long Beach handled 919,992 TEU in August 2026, its busiest August in the port's 115-year history and up 2% year on year. Combined LA-Long Beach volume ran near 1.88 million TEU, also close to a record.
