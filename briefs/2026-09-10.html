Freight Forwarding / NVOCC

Samsung’s FMC action against CMA CGM tests whether OSRA invoice rules protect shippers when terminal gates refuse empties

A demurrage dispute is no longer an informal commercial negotiation; it is a statutory document audit. Samsung Electronics America has filed a formal complaint with the Federal Maritime Commission against CMA CGM S.A., alleging unlawful detention and demurrage charges incurred during periods of terminal gridlock where empty container return appointments were unavailable.

Truckload Brokerage

FMCSA’s enforcement freeze on biennial updates leaves broker onboarding systems blind to zombie carrier authorities

When a safety regulator pauses authority deactivations, automated compliance software stops being a reliable shield. The Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) has temporarily suspended scheduled motor carrier deactivations for entities failing to submit biennial MCS-150 updates, citing technical data synchronization backlogs during its ongoing MOTUS IT modernization rollout.

Asset Carriage / Truckload Fleet

Record $5.97 diesel tests whether motor carrier fuel escalators can recover double-digit input spikes before quarterly cash depletion

A fuel price spike is not a demand signal; it is an immediate balance-sheet liquidity test. The U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) national on-highway retail diesel price rose to $5.967/gallon for reference date 2026-09-07, with California reaching $7.764/gal and the East Coast $5.744/gal.

Freight Forwarding / NVOCC

Indonesia’s INSW expansion into domestic cargo signals the beginning of regulatory digital enclosure across inter-island trade

The informal era of archipelagic freight documentation is closing. The Indonesian National Single Window (Lembaga National Single Window / INSW) has officially expanded its statutory integration from international export-import clearance into domestic trade and inter-island cargo monitoring.
