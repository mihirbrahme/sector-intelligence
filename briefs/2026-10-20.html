Truckload Brokerage

Carrier pricing power is narrowing to truckload capacity alone, and the intermodal leg of the same cycle is already giving ground

2026-10-19 read thinning for-hire capacity and a record import surge as the setup for a broker margin squeeze under manual carrier sourcing under rate volatility. The most current composite freight-pricing data available this session shows that tightening staying truckload-specific, while the intermodal half of the same index is already reversing. FreightWaves' SONAR Pricing Power Index fell to 70 from 71, its fourth consecutive weekly decline, in the reading its own coverage places around 2026-10-12 (FreightWaves/SONAR, restated via WebSearch; exact publication date not located this session — freightwaves.com and sonar.freightwaves.com both refused the proxy CONNECT tunnel on direct fetch; for the index by construction, for this session's restatement). The single largest drag was the intermodal contract-rate component, down to $1.61 a mile from $1.68, which dropped it from the 54th to the 36th percentile of its own history.

Energy shipping: LNG, crude, product, offshore FPSO/FSO

The Strait of Hormuz tanker campaign is still escalating eight months in, not settling into a stable risk premium

this record last logged the Hormuz war in 2026-10-06, mainly as a driver of elevated container spot rates. Attack data from the following two weeks argue the campaign itself is intensifying, something this record has not tracked directly since. UK Maritime Trade Operations issued four separate attack reports on 2026-10-11 covering three tankers and a container ship, and a container ship took a hit on an outbound transit on 2026-10-13 with only minor damage reported. Maritime-security sources cited in the same coverage counted at least 12 tanker attacks in the week of 2026-09-28 to 2026-10-05, the highest weekly total since the war began on 2026-02-28, and a cited IMO tally stood at 106 confirmed incidents as of 2026-10-13 (same sourcing; for the underlying counts if the attribution holds, for this session's unverified restatement — imo.org was not reached directly).
