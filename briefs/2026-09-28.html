Truckload Brokerage

Truckload capacity is turning on demand recovery, not driver scarcity, and that points to a cycle near its top

Three prior briefs debated whether truckload capacity was tight because carriers had genuinely exited, or only looked tight while spot rates drifted down. New data reframes the question: demand is recovering and carriers are ordering trucks again, which is what happens once a tightening cycle is already priced in, not what starts one. Cass Freight Index shipments rose 2.1% year-on-year in August, to a reading of 1.038 — the first annual gain since January 2023, ending a 42-month downturn described as the longest on record. The Cass Truckload Linehaul Index rose 11.3% year-on-year in the same month, a 20th consecutive increase and the largest since June 2022 (same source and grades).

Asset Carriage / Truckload Fleet

CVH Transport's 49 years breaks the young-company insolvency pattern this record flagged last week

The 2026-09-24 brief read September's UK haulage failures as an entry-cost problem landing on young operators. A confirmed failure this month is the opposite kind of company, and it is the most striking of the four. CVH Transport Services, incorporated 1977-05-02 and trading from 1973, entered creditors' voluntary liquidation on 2026-09-16 after 49 years. That sits beside three younger September CVLs: Spareryb Logistics (incorporated 2022-05, liquidated 2026-09-03), RP Bulk Haulage (incorporated 2024-09, liquidated 2026-09-04), and Davis-Wright Haulage (incorporated 2020-08-17, liquidated 2026-09-02).

LTL

Old Dominion, not Saia, is already showing the falsifier last week's LTL judgment asked for

The 2026-09-25 brief said its "carriers are pricing ahead of demand" reading would fail if Q3 tonnage at Old Dominion and Saia fell even as rates rose. Old Dominion's own August data already meets that condition. Saia's does not. Old Dominion's LTL tons per day fell 0.9% year-on-year in August — shipments per day down 2.4%, partly offset by weight per shipment up 1.7% — while revenue per day rose 12.4% on higher LTL revenue per hundredweight (Old Dominion Freight Line, Form 8-K exhibit, filed 2026-09-03; ).
