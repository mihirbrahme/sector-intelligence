LTL

ArcBest's Q3 margin gain, won on a network barely touched by the Yellow terminal auction, closes the LTL discriminator this record has been building since Q3 earnings

ArcBest is now the third of four reporting Q3 2026 LTL carriers to convert the quarter's volume recovery into margin. The fourth, Saia, is the one still absorbing new ex-Yellow terminal capacity. That split is the discriminator 2026-11-13 named and 2026-11-16 sharpened with XPO. ArcBest closes it, because its own post-Yellow network barely grew.

Ports & Terminals

A fourth deadline extension in the LA/Long Beach watchmen dispute reads as a negotiation converging on a deal, not one heading for a strike

My reading is that the repeated extension of this contract deadline is itself the signal, and it points toward settlement rather than disruption. A dispute that keeps getting a new date, with both sides still at the table, is not the same shape as one heading toward a walkout. Local 26, roughly 250 watchmen handling security-related terminal access at Los Angeles and Long Beach, and the Pacific Maritime Association have pushed their bargaining deadline four times in under three weeks: from November 1 to November 4, to midnight November 6, and now to November 20. The Federal Mediation and Conciliation Service is assisting both sides.
