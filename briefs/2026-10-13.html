Mobile's actual closure under Hurricane Isaias was the Coast Guard's most severe port condition, not the one this record logged two days ago

2026-10-12 read the Mobile-area closure through Port Condition Yankee — a 24-hour advisory step, not a full stop. Post-landfall reporting available today says the actual designation was Port Condition Zulu, the Coast Guard's highest level, under which a port suspends vessel movement entirely. That is a harder closure than Monday's brief characterised, even though the storm's own track came ashore well east of Mobile. Isaias made landfall near Destin, Florida, as a Category 2 storm Friday night into Saturday, roughly 150 miles east of Mobile Bay.
