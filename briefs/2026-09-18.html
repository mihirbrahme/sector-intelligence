Asset Carriage / Truckload Fleet

Truckload capacity is tightening on driver-pool removal, not a demand recovery, and Cass's own numbers say so

Cass's August print looks like the demand turn the market has waited three and a half years for. It reads better as a capacity story: shipments barely moved while price moved hard, and the driver pool shrank by regulation, not by business failure. Cass Freight Index Shipments rose to 1.038 in August 2026, up 2.1% year on year — the first year-on-year gain after 42 straight months of decline. Expenditures rose to 3.722, up 18.7% year on year, and the Cass Truckload Linehaul Index rose 11.3% year on year to 153.9.

LTL

XPO's August growth makes Old Dominion the outlier, not the read this record gave three days ago

The 2026-09-17 brief corrected its own 2026-09-14 read once, from "share moving toward scale carriers" to "Old Dominion's decline is a pricing choice, not a demand signal." XPO's own August data says that correction went too far the other way. Old Dominion looks like the outlier among three majors, not the network-wide signal. XPO's LTL tonnage per day rose 3.7% year on year in August 2026, on shipments per day up 5.7% and weight per shipment down 1.8%.

Freight Forwarding / NVOCC

CBP's new power to void an importer number on the spot raises the price of a customs broker's data-entry mistake

A customs entry data error has always cost time. From today it can cost the importer its ability to import at all, with no warning and no cure period. That changes what "close enough" data capture is worth to a customs brokerage. CBP's new enforcement authority, effective 2026-09-18, lets it void an importer of record (IOR) number immediately on finding inaccurate or incomplete Form 5106 data, barring that importer from entering merchandise until the number is restored.
