International container liner and feeder shipping

The USTR vessel-fee suspension was extended inside its own deadline, closing the gap this record flagged twice

The Section 301 suspension on Chinese-linked vessel fees was extended, and extended before its deadline lapsed, not after. 2026-11-09 and 2026-11-10 both called extension the likely outcome but both flagged the same risk: the diplomatic agreement might outrun the legal instrument needed to give it effect. That risk did not materialise. A Federal Register notice, "Notice of Modification of Section 301 Action: China's Targeting of the Maritime, Logistics, and Shipbuilding Sectors for Dominance" (FR Doc. 2026-22867), published 2026-11-10.

Asset Carriage / Truckload Fleet

Knight-Swift's Q3 margin recovery is a Truckload story, and the consolidated figure cited in yesterday's brief was never the Truckload number

2026-11-10 read Knight-Swift's 89.3% adjusted operating ratio as the Truckload segment's own figure. this record's own company file already held the correct number: Truckload's Q3 2026 adjusted OR was 89.2%, a 700-basis-point improvement, and 89.3% is the consolidated figure across Truckload, LTL and Logistics. The consolidated figure understates how lopsided the quarter was. Intermodal revenue rose 30.6% year over year on loads up 13.3%, the highest load growth in more than five years, but its adjusted OR was 97.8% — barely profitable even after a 200-basis-point improvement.

Asset Carriage / Truckload Fleet

UK road haulage rates turned down in October, the opposite signal from the US truckload tightening story

US truckload capacity is tightening into the 2027 bid season, per the Knight-Swift reading above and 2026-11-10. UK road haulage moved the opposite way in October. The TEG Road Transport Price Index fell 0.42% to 141.0 points, its first monthly fall since February, on a 9.98% drop in demand and an 11.55% rise in vehicle availability. Prices remain 10.85% above a year earlier, so this is a cooling from an elevated base, not a collapse.
