Inland liquid-bulk marine / tank barge

The Port of Mobile's Hurricane Isaias closure ran roughly two days under full vessel suspension, not the "a few hours" test this record set on Monday

2026-10-13 read Mobile's closure through Coast Guard Port Condition Zulu and set an explicit test: the severity label would not matter if Zulu "lasted only a few hours before stepping down — a precaution many Gulf ports take even for storms tracking away from them." That test has now run, and it runs the other way. The Alabama Port Authority's pre-storm notice set public-berth vessel operations to stop at noon Thursday 2026-10-08, with terminal operations suspended by end of that day. Isaias made landfall near Destin, Florida, after dark on Friday 2026-10-09 into Saturday 2026-10-10.

Asset Carriage / Truckload Fleet

EIA's own October forecast undercuts the "sustained cost regime" framing this record flagged on 2026-10-09 — this is a reversion story even after the agency revised its forecast upward

2026-10-09 logged C.H. Robinson's October Edge Report framing September's $6.53/gal diesel peak as the opening of a cost regime running "well into next year," citing EIA's own distillate-inventory outlook. That brief could only test the claim against the record's weekly series, which was easing but is still a market read. EIA's own multi-year forecast, now available, is a sharper test, and it does not support a sustained-regime reading. EIA's October 2026 Short-Term Energy Outlook, published via EIA press release around 2026-10-06/07 raised its retail diesel forecast to $5.19/gal for full-year 2026 (from $5.07 in September's edition) and to $4.49/gal for 2027 (from $4.40).
