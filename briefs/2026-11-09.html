LTL

ArcBest's Q3 2026 figures, read directly, contradict the "LTL and brokerage woes" headline — the real soft spot is narrower than either prior reading had it

Three straight briefs carried an unresolved conflict between ArcBest's own Q3 2026 figures and a FreightWaves "woes" framing. GAAP diluted EPS was $2.30, up from $1.72; adjusted diluted EPS was $2.62, up from $1.46 and ahead of consensus near $2.51–$2.54. Asset-Based (LTL) revenue rose 9.9% to $798.6 million, operating income to $75.6 million from $70.2 million, at a 90.5% operating ratio. Shipments per day fell 3.0% while weight per shipment rose 13.0% and billed revenue per hundredweight rose only 1.1% — ex-fuel yield fell in the low single digits.
