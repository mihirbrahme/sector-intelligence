Asset Carriage / Truckload Fleet

UK haulage failures are landing on young companies, not ageing fleets

Two hauliers that appointed liquidators in the same week were two and four years old. That points at an entry-cost problem for new operators, not a fleet running out of road. Spareryb Logistics (Milton Keynes), incorporated May 2022, appointed liquidators 2026-09-03. RP Bulk Haulage (Lancashire), incorporated September 2024, appointed liquidators 2026-09-04.

Freight Forwarding / NVOCC

Air freight yield is not keeping pace with jet fuel, and the shortfall is landing on the transpacific lane

Air cargo pricing has moved a fraction of what its main cost input has, and the gap is not spread evenly across lanes. The Baltic Air Freight Index (BAI00) rose 0.9% over the week to 2026-09-21 and sits 20.9% above a year earlier. Over the same window, IATA's Jet Fuel Price Monitor read jet fuel up 116.5% year on year to 2026-09-18 (same restatement chain; by construction, here). China-Europe rates rose week on week as e-commerce volume recovers from the EU's de minimis exemption ending in July, while China-US rates eased slightly week on week even with transpacific yields still up year on year.

Truckload Brokerage

The one series that would settle the capacity debate is eleven days stale

2026-09-23 flagged a missing tender-rejection figure needed to test whether truckload capacity is genuinely tight. That figure exists, but it stopped updating before the data it needs to be tested against. The national Outbound Tender Rejection Index held at 13.45% as of 2026-09-10, "significantly above the same period in prior years," after a Labor Day-week spike above 14.5%. DAT's dry van spot rate then fell in three of the next four weekly prints through 2026-09-21, per 2026-09-23.
