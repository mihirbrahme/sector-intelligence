Asset Carriage / Truckload Fleet

Werner's margin gain is confirmed capacity discipline, overshot by its own account, not the undisclosed Dedicated mix shift flagged twice before

2026-10-28 and 2026-10-30 flagged Werner's and Schneider's blended Truckload segment gains as possibly a Dedicated mix shift rather than genuine capacity discipline, with the One-Way (Network) sub-segment split undisclosed in both cases. 2026-11-02 repeated the gap for both companies. Werner's own Q3 2026 release and earnings call, located this session, close it for Werner specifically: the One-Way fleet was cut deliberately, and the company says it cut more than it meant to. Werner's One-Way Truckload average trucks fell 37% year over year to 1,673, while One-Way revenue net of fuel fell 15% to $136 million.

Ports & Terminals

A 250-worker ILWU craft local, not the coastwide contract, is the live disruption risk at LA/Long Beach

The headline risk at the twin ports is not the coastwide ILWU longshore and clerks agreement, which runs through 2028 and is not in dispute. It is a narrow contract covering roughly 250 security watchmen, whose expired agreement has been extended twice in four days. Because other ILWU members will not cross a Local 26 picket line, a strike by this small craft unit can stop work at terminals handling a disproportionate share of the complex's cargo — a leverage point the coastwide deal's stability obscures. ILWU Local 26 watchmen, who monitor terminal access and cargo security, work at terminals handling approximately 85% of Long Beach's cargo and 33% of Los Angeles's, according to the Pacific Maritime Association.

Truckload Brokerage

Landstar's insurance and claims costs grew nearly three times faster than revenue, extending carrier-selection liability repricing the cost of cheap capacity's evidence trail into a third quarter

Landstar's Q3 looks like a blowout quarter on the headline numbers, but inside it is a claims cost line still accelerating under carrier-selection liability repricing the cost of cheap capacity, the carrier-selection liability mechanism the record already tracks at this company. The mechanism is intensifying, not stabilising, even as earnings grow. Landstar reported Q3 2026 revenue of $1.512 billion, up 25% year over year, with truck segment revenue of $1.407 billion, up 29%, on revenue per load up roughly 25% and loads up roughly 3%. Operating income rose to $65.5 million from $26.3 million and diluted EPS rose to $1.42 from $0.56.
