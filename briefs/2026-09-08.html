Truckload Brokerage

Mexicali border blockade over B-1 visa revocations shows a new capacity risk at the US-Mexico crossing, not yet in the record

Roughly 300 Mexican truck drivers blockaded the Mexicali and San Luis Río Colorado commercial crossings from 2026-09-03 to 2026-09-07, after US inspectors revoked B-1 visas from at least 17 drivers on suspected cabotage (a B-1 holder may move international cargo between Mexico and the US but not haul domestic US freight once there). Protesters warned the blockade could extend to Tijuana's Otay Mesa crossing, which handled close to $60 billion of US-Mexico freight last year.
