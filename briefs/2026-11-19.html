Truckload Brokerage

Freight spend is now outrunning freight volume four to one, and that is the rate cycle manual carrier sourcing under rate volatility has been waiting on

Cass's October data describe a market where price, not tonnage, is doing almost all the work. That is the precondition manual carrier sourcing under rate volatility has been waiting on. It is not yet the test itself. The Cass Freight Index shipments reading was 1.056 for October 2026, up 5.9% year over year — the second straight year-over-year gain after a 3.5-year downturn.

Freight Forwarding / NVOCC

The FMC's Peloton ruling sets a container-by-container evidence bar that favours forwarders, not shippers, on detention and demurrage

This ruling makes a customer's detention-and-demurrage claim against its own forwarder harder to win, by requiring proof tied to one container and one day rather than an aggregate billing pattern. It is the first evidence this record holds on a liability question detention and demurrage under-recovery explicitly flags as unwritten, and it answers that question in the forwarder's favour. An FMC administrative law judge's initial decision, served 2026-09-09 in Docket No. 24-19, rejected Peloton Interactive's $33.7 million claim against Flexport International — $13.8m demurrage, $16.2m equipment detention, $3.7m storage. The judge held the FMC's 2024 billing rule contains no bar on charging D&D for store-door moves, and required container-specific, day-specific evidence tying each charge to unreasonable conduct, citing the same standard applied in Samsung Electronics America v. SM Line.

Domestic Intermodal

BNSF's numbered rebuttal and a quoted threat from Union Pacific's own CEO point the UP-NS merger toward a contested, conditions-heavy review

The breadth and specificity of opposition filed by the STB's deadline argue against a clean approval. Union Pacific's own chief executive handed opponents their sharpest exhibit, which is not the position a party heading toward an easy win hands the other side. By the Surface Transportation Board's 2026-11-18 deadline for comments, protests and responsive applications in Finance Docket No. FD 36873, BNSF filed formal opposition identifying more than 310 claimed errors in the applicants' traffic analysis, and stated that shipper groups representing over 50% of rail freight volume and unions representing over 70% of the projected combined workforce oppose or have raised concerns.
