Asset Carriage / Truckload Fleet

The administration's own count of English-proficiency enforcement actions has no stable definition, and that is now a regulatory problem, not just a sourcing one

FMCSA's English-language-proficiency out-of-service rule (docket FMCSA-2026-0826) closes its public comment window today, 2026-10-09. Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy is using this week's publicity to cite "more than 26,000" drivers removed from the road — a figure that does not reconcile with any number this programme has produced before it. The chain, in sequence: FMCSA's own Analysis & Information database showed 1,212 out-of-service actions in August 2025; Duffy's own public statement the same month said roughly 1,500, a gap one CVSA official attributed to state reporting lag. By September 2025 one count reached 3,020; by October 2025 inspectors had issued 6,455 proficiency violations but placed only 1,816 drivers out of service — violations and out-of-service orders being different things (same sourcing).

Asset Carriage / Truckload Fleet

The diesel figure driving freight-cost forecasts into 2027 is already two weeks behind the government's own series

C.H. Robinson's October 2026 market update frames September's diesel spike as the opening of a cost regime running into 2027. The benchmark register's own weekly series, tracking the same fuel, already shows that spike receding. EIA's weekly on-highway diesel price, the series this record carries — peaked at $6.529/gal for the week ending 2026-09-21, eased to $6.382 for the week ending 2026-09-28, and to $6.199 for the week ending 2026-10-05, all logged in 2026-10-07. C.H. Robinson's own October Edge Report calls September "a record high of $6.53" and forecasts the pressure continuing "well into next year," citing EIA's own distillate-inventory outlook through much of 2027.

Truckload Brokerage

The CHRW-RXO deal's shareholder vote is turning out to be the easy condition; the integration risk this record already doubted is still the hard one

2026-10-06 listed RXO shareholder approval among the deal's remaining conditions, alongside the carrier-master integration risk this record doubted from the start. The approval condition looks largely secured. The integration risk does not. Under a voting and support agreement signed with the merger agreement, MFN Partners LP — holding roughly 17.04% of RXO's outstanding shares — agreed to vote those shares for the deal, a commitment that lapses only if RXO's own board withdraws its recommendation.
