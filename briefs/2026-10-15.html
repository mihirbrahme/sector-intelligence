Asset Carriage / Truckload Fleet

The diesel "sustained cost regime" thesis keeps failing the test this record set for it, and Hurricane Isaias removed the one live supply shock that could have rescued it

2026-10-14 set an explicit test for C.H. Robinson Worldwide, Inc.'s Edge Report framing of September's diesel peak as a sustained cost regime rather than a reversion: the week-ending-2026-10-12 EIA reading would either resume the decline logged since the 2026-09-21 peak or climb back toward it. It has now resumed the decline, and a second thread closes in the same direction — the Gulf refineries Hurricane Isaias put at risk came through undamaged. EIA's national average on-highway diesel price for the week ending 2026-10-12 was $6.179/gal, down $0.02 from $6.199 for the week ending 2026-10-05 — a third consecutive weekly decline from the $6.529 peak of 2026-09-21. benchmark register still carries the 2026-10-05 reading as its most recently verified row; this figure is not entered there because it could not be confirmed against the publisher this session.

Domestic Intermodal

The STB's refusal to summarily dismiss the UP–NS merger application settles nothing about the merger's merits, and the parties who sought dismissal are positioned to re-raise the same objection at the November comment deadline

The Surface Transportation Board's 2026-09-18 denial of motions to summarily deny Union Pacific and Norfolk Southern's revised merger application reads, in trade coverage, as the merger clearing a hurdle. It is better read as the Board declining to decide the case early, which is a different thing and leaves the underlying objection unresolved rather than settled. The motions, filed around 2026-08-18 to 2026-08-20 by competing Class I railroads, a shipper trade-association coalition and a group of state attorneys general, argued the revised application was incomplete and premature. The Board's 2026-05-28 acceptance of the application already stated that acceptance "does not constitute a ruling on the merits" (benchmark register, verified 2026-09-09); the September denial repeats that same caveat rather than ruling on completeness itself.
