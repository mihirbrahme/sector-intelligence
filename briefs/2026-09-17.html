LTL

Saia is taking LTL tonnage while Old Dominion's own network is shrinking, which corrects a read this record made three days ago

The 2026-09-14 brief read Old Dominion's operating update as volumes "nearing positive territory." Old Dominion's own August data says the opposite, and the divergence with Saia is sharper than the record knew. Old Dominion reported LTL tons per day down 0.9% year on year in August 2026, even as revenue per day rose 12.4% on higher revenue per hundredweight (Old Dominion 8-K operating-metrics exhibit, filed 2026-09-03; — the company's own unaudited monthly release). Saia reported LTL tonnage per workday up 7.8% in July and 8.7% in August, both year on year (Saia release via GlobeNewswire, 2026-09-03;, same class).

International container liner and feeder shipping

The Suez return has stopped being one alliance's bet and become an industry-wide capacity release

The 2026-09-15 and 2026-09-16 briefs treated Gemini's return to Suez as a single alliance's wager against Red Sea risk. New canal data says the wager is now most of the industry's, which changes the size of the capacity effect and the number of carriers exposed to a reversal. The Suez Canal Authority reported container-ship net tonnage transiting the canal at 72.1 million tons for January–August 2026, up 54.2% from 46.7 million tons in the same period of 2025. On 2026-09-16 the 24,188-TEU OOCL Portugal became COSCO's first vessel to transit the canal since the Red Sea crisis began, alongside continuing transits by CMA CGM, MSC and Hapag-Lloyd.

Domestic Intermodal

UP-NS's 500-shipper support campaign answers the merits question rivals aren't the ones asking right now

Union Pacific and Norfolk Southern are contesting the merger fight on the ground BNSF, CPKC and CSX have not chosen to fight it on. That mismatch matters more than either filing alone. UP and NS's own release states more than 500 shippers, including 23 new supporters from agriculture, energy, fertilizer, forest products, food and automotive, back the merger, and that shifting freight from long-haul trucking to rail would save shippers $3.5 billion annually. Separately and still unresolved, BNSF, CPKC and CSX's 2026-09-14 letter — covered in the 2026-09-15 brief — asks the STB to freeze further amendments and force certification by 2026-09-19; no board ruling on that request had been reported as of this search.
