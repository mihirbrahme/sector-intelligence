Freight Forwarding / NVOCC

Kuehne+Nagel's Amazon deal is a forwarder buying its way into contract logistics, not a freight-volume story

Kuehne+Nagel signed a long-term collaboration with Amazon on 2026-09-21 that runs well past forwarding. The deal covers construction, equipment deployment, maintenance and upgrades across the AWS data-centre lifecycle. It extends K+N's role beyond air into ocean, road and contract logistics for the same customer. Amazon holds a call option on K+N shares, vesting against commercial milestones over up to seven years.

Domestic Intermodal

The Surface Transportation Board just made the UP-NS merger harder to kill early

The STB denied three motions for summary denial of the Union Pacific-Norfolk Southern merger application on 2026-09-18. BNSF, CSX and a shipper coalition including the American Chemistry Council and the National Industrial Transportation League had filed them. The Board said the denial reflects no view on the merits, only that "additional evidence and argument will aid its decision-making in this consequential transaction of first impression." Opening comments are now due 2026-11-18, responses 2027-02-16.
