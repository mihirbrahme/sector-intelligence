Truckload Brokerage

September's spot-rate slide argues against the driver-removal capacity story, not for it

If driver-pool removal were tightening capacity the way the 2026-09-18 brief read it, spot rates should be firming through September, not fading. They are fading. DAT's national average dry van spot linehaul rate, ex-fuel, ran $2.19/mile (week of 2026-09-01), $2.21 (2026-09-08, +1.1%), $2.20 (2026-09-15, −0.6%) and $2.17 (2026-09-21, −1.2%) — a net decline from the month's peak across three of four weekly prints. The rate still sits 32.8% above the same week of 2025 and 19.7% above DAT's nine-year seasonal average, so this is a retreat from an elevated base, not a collapse.

Domestic Intermodal

The diesel spike J.B. Hunt called "cyclical" is still climbing three weeks on, and the record missed the event that named it

J.B. Hunt's CFO told investors a record fuel spike was "more cyclical than structural." The one series this record can check independently of J.B. Hunt has kept climbing every week since, which argues the other way.

Freight Forwarding / NVOCC

CBP's September ANPRM shows the September 18 importer-number rule was the opening move, not the whole programme

The 2026-09-18 brief logged CBP's power to void an importer-of-record number on a Form 5106 error and asked, in its own Gaps, whether other agencies were issuing companion enforcement under the same executive order. Part of that answer has surfaced: CBP itself is going further under its own authority. On 2026-09-02, CBP issued an advance notice of proposed rulemaking, "Heightened Import Disclosures for Supply Chain Visibility," under Executive Order 14411. It targets three areas: fuller identification of parties involved in an importation, integration of technical cargo-tracing solutions, and collection of foreign export documentation.
